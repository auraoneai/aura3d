# Skyline Runner — Relay Across the Dawn Redesign

**Route:** `apps/showcase-skyline-runner/`  
**Claim boundary:** `createAuraApp` root safe API plus route-local platformer content; no universal platformer-quality claim

## Player promise

Carry a relay spark across three rooftop districts before dawn breaks: leap, dash, collect, defeat hazards, activate relays, and chase the silhouette of your best run. The route should read as a deliberate side-on journey through changing places, not a line of floating platforms.

## Preserve

- Certified/generated level geometry and proven 70–115 second completion window remain intact unless a separately approved recertification changes them.
- Public platformer state, checkpoints, hazards, score, lives, completion, pause, and reset stay authoritative.
- Typed runner/world assets remain the primary subjects; primitives support certified play surfaces and abstract effects only.

## Creative redesign

### Three districts

1. **Steel Dawn:** cool roofs, antennas, cranes, long readable jumps; teaches movement and first relay.
2. **Hanging Grove:** rooftop gardens, instanced foliage, warmer mist, moving visual layers; introduces tighter hazard timing.
3. **Crown Heights:** gold sunrise, tall silhouettes, final sentry pressure, longest gap, and finish beacon.

Each district owns a palette, ambience stem, skyline silhouette, one landmark, and one mechanical emphasis. `distanceLod` controls distant architecture; instancing handles repeated foliage/coins where it improves draw behavior. Backgrounds never masquerade as collision surfaces.

### Camera and readability

- Stable side-on/three-quarter camera leads the player's facing direction and respects vertical jump space.
- Player, safe platforms, hazards, collectibles, relay sensors, and finish each use consistent shape plus color language.
- Foreground framing never crosses the player's collision silhouette or hides landing edges.
- Ghost runner is translucent, outline/value-distinct, and non-interactive.

### Feel and arc

Jump anticipation is subtle; land, dash, collect, relay, hazard, defeat, respawn, and finish each have distinct state-tied scene/audio responses. Mesh-text district gates can announce names using supported glyphs, while DOM supplies accessible objective and timer information. Final relay activation shifts skyline light and opens the finish composition.
