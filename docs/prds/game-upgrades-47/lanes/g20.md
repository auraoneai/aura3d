## Decisions

- `?look=original` keeps the whole pre-GU47 rig, post chain, aces and sky constants (the sky shader defaults equal the old constants).
- The visible sky is the authored dome, so `environment.background` is `"sky"`; `belfast_sunset_puresky` is IBL only (secondary use, no look-identity clash).
- Look values are applied on a 3 m step with short engine-side blends rather than every frame, so patches stay cheap.
