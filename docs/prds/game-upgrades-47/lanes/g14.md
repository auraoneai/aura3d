## CHANGELOG text (for WP-38)

Cross Hop: a tabletop toy diorama through a tilt-shift lens. Each of the six themes is an hour of one day (dawn, overcast noon, golden hour, lava-lit dusk, lamplit night, studio toybox) with its own sky, light HDRI, height fog, post preset and one shadowed sun that moves as you hop. The focus band follows your critter; critters keep their own textures; new landing, splash, spark and per-death effects; a tabletop HUD. `?look=original` shows the previous look. Numbers are software WebGL2 and are measured in the phase-2 capture.
