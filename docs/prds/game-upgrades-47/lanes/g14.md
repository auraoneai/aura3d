### The vivid look

The diorama lost every view to its steep camera, so phase 3 adds a third look, `vivid` (`src/game/look.ts` `VIVID_LOOKS`, `core.ts` `buildVividScene`). It keeps the 4.6 chase camera, lanes, models and critter material, and changes only:

- one shadowed sun from the camera's left (2 camera-fitted cascades) plus a hemisphere fill, instead of the flat ambient and two weak directionals;
- a gradient sky (stars at night, light clouds by day) with a low horizon glow, instead of the two flat sky boxes and the beige sun sphere;
- the world floor laid flat in the theme's meadow colour (the 4.6 floor stood upright, so a sky-coloured band filled the frame bottom);
- a per-theme grade and bloom, and distance haze only at dusk and night;
- river logs in lacquered terracotta with butter-yellow ends (identity palette; the sibling judge matched the old bark-brown logs with cloud-runner-port).

Default per view: `defaultLookFor(lookParam, theme)`. An explicit `?look=vivid|original|diorama` always wins; otherwise the starting theme decides through `VIVID_DEFAULT_THEMES`. Quick Play, World Tour and the final screens start on theme 0, so they open in theme 0's look. The look is chosen once at boot; a run that cycles into another theme keeps its boot look.
