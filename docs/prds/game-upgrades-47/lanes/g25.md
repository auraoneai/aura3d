## Decisions

- Run 2: the waterfall sits at s=150 on the right side, where the profile is a full drop (cliffMode 1, no canyon) in the first 290 m, so it is seen early in every run. The DOF/blur effects are authored once and toggled by the per-frame plan (only changed states are written) rather than rebuilt.

- Sun azimuth 52 deg kept from today (side-front over the prevailing heading), elevation dropped to 28.
- `spruit_sunrise` is IBL only (background off); the custom dome stays the primary visible environment, so the look-identity HDRI-duplicate rule does not bite. Rotation 140 deg differs from other users.
- Rainbow, towers and rain curtains live in the dome shader: 0 draws and they survive Low.
- Practicals: 1 (the rim) in the direction; the audit mirror has one shadowed key plus the rim.
