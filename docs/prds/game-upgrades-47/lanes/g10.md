## Decisions

- No interim adapters: the wave-1 predecessor's `art/engineCompat.ts` (child patching for E-A keys) is deleted; `game/lookRig.ts` calls `looks.preset(base, overrides)` with the merged E-A keys.
- `courseFill` stays the 4.6.2 rig, used only by `?look=original` and the existing light-budget test (`course-lighting.test.ts`), so that test's premise is unchanged.
- Neon Bay's key is the floodlight bank (warm white, shadowed), the moon becomes its rim; the four 4.6.2 lamp spots become two mast spots at the back pylons (`?look=original` keeps the four).
- Masts stay unshadowed: the art-direction audit allows one shadowed light for `golf-physics`; PRD LINKS-16 asks for two shadowed masts. One-line switch (`shadow: true` in `lookRig.ts`) once the audit admits shadowed practicals for night venues (handoff below).
- HDRI rotation: the engine samples equirect maps at u = atan2(z, x)/2pi + 0.5 + rotation (turns); rotation = course sun azimuth - the HDRI's brightest-texel azimuth. Phase 2 checks the drawn sun against shadows.
- `?look=original` keeps light/atmosphere/post and the 4.6.2 juice particles, trail dots and authored ribbon; runtime VFX are on in both modes.
