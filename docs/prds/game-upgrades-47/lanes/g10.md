## Decisions

- No interim adapters: the wave-1 predecessor's `art/engineCompat.ts` (child patching for E-A keys) is deleted; `game/lookRig.ts` calls `looks.preset(base, overrides)` with the merged E-A keys.

- Neon Bay's key is the floodlight bank (warm white, shadowed), the moon becomes its rim; the four 4.6.2 lamp spots become two mast spots at the back pylons (`?look=original` keeps the four).
- Masts stay unshadowed: the art-direction audit allows one shadowed light for `golf-physics`; PRD LINKS-16 asks for two shadowed masts. One-line switch (`shadow: true` in `lookRig.ts`) once the audit admits shadowed practicals for night venues (handoff below).
- HDRI rotation: the engine samples equirect maps at u = atan2(z, x)/2pi + 0.5 + rotation (turns); rotation = course sun azimuth - the HDRI's brightest-texel azimuth. Phase 2 checks the drawn sun against shadows.
- `?look=original` keeps light/atmosphere/post and the 4.6.2 juice particles, trail dots and authored ribbon; runtime VFX are on in both modes.

- LINKS-05 textures are generated in code (no files): no CC0 photo set is in the repo for turf, and downloads plus admission would exceed phase 1; the slots (`TURF_SURFACES`) take an admitted `assets.x` texture later with no mesh change.
- LINKS-17 uses `world.water` rather than `surfaces.mirror` (see row). The PRD's "<= 2 mirrors at 256 px" holds as "<= 2 planar water reflections per hole"; the capture resolution is the water runtime's, measured in phase 2.

### Shadows: root cause

Cast shadows did not show in any capture, although `app.diagnostics().lighting.shadows` reported the sun map rendered
and sampled with 3 PCSS cascades (run `p4-x1`). Cause: the engine's default 4.2.0 ("legacy") sun fit widens every
cascade to every caster's bounds, and this course's instanced trees, rocks and tufts span the whole 400 m hole, so each
2048 px cascade covered the hole and the golfer's shadow fell to about one texel (same mechanism as
`docs/audits/lanes/e3.md`; the 4.7.1 Diamond frames show it too). Fix (d70d8fd29): the GU47 look opts into the 4.3.0 fit
(`__A3D_SHADOW_FIT__ = "current"`, as Shadowstep Gauntlet and Ninja Runner); `?look=original` and `?shadowfit=legacy`
keep the 4.2.0 fit. No real-GPU frame of the 4.3.0 fit exists for this game yet (owner check). On top: stronger course
suns over dimmer skies on the four HDRI day courses (780e5a007), so the shadow contrast holds against the IBL fill.
