## Decisions

- Genre `basketball` in the art direction: `GameGenre` has no football entry (handoff E-J).
- Turf texture only on the grass venues (Munich, Kyoto); Favela dirt, Lagos sand and Uyuni salt stay flat colour, Glacier is clearcoat ice. Look row gains `surface` and `mirror`.
- Stands are built per venue (the scene is rebuilt on venue change, FC-21), so the batch holds only visible tiers; applyVenue re-sets instances if it ever runs without a rebuild.
- HUD fonts registered with FontFace rather than `registerGameFonts`: the app has no `@aura3d/game` dependency and adding one needs a lockfile change.
- The unused borrowed keys in `aura-assets-v2.ts` (rooftop*, hoopsAthlete*, unused SFX) stay: `tests/unit/likeness-audit/*` and `skin-validate.test.ts` read the manifest, and the replacement belongs to WP-36.
