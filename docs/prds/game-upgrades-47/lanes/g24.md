## CHANGELOG text (WP-38)

Duneglide (silt-and-signal-port): seven chapters now read as seven hours of one long day, each with its own key
direction and colour, an inked sky dome with the sun or moon on the same vector, height-fog ground haze, ACES grading
and a depth plus colour ink pass that fades out by 600 m. A sand wake follows the board at speed and low altitude,
twin jade ribbons trail it, ring passes flash jade, and every chapter has its own ambient motes. The HUD is a poster
HUD in licensed Graphik and Canela with a brass compass. `?look=original` keeps the previous look. Measured on
software WebGL2 in phase 2.

## Phase-2 visual fix

Judged by eye at settle-60s against the 4.6.2 frames (wp38 `before/`, and the recap2 `chN-original` frames, which use the
unchanged original path), software WebGL2 on the remote runner (two rounds: `fix/` = round 1, `fix2/` = round 2).

**Root cause of the bad recap2 frames:** the DG-C1 intro rail counted down on the loop's clamped frame time. At a few
frames per second in software WebGL2 the 4 s rail lasted minutes, so every inked capture at 25 s and 60 s was still
mid-rail (camera high and to the side, inside rings) with sequence DOF on. The rail and the DG-C3 finish orbit now also
advance on wall-clock time (`main.ts` `sequenceStep`), 19f7ef5d6. Same commit: height fog density x0.6 and max opacity
0.55 in every chapter, softer ink bands (bandMix 0.5 day / 0.45 night), inked water sun glint x3 -> x1.2 and fresnel
0.55 -> 0.3 (the dawn canal was blown to white). Round 2 (3d6c85908): ch1 bluer sky, less dust and haze, lower exposure;
ch3 and ch4 lifted (exposure, contrast, lighter fog); ch6 less pink haze, sky glow and bloom.
