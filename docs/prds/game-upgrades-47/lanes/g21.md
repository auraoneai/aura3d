## Decisions

- Practicals: the C-35 audit counts every non-key light against `practicals` (max 6) and allows one shadowed light for racing. The WP's 26 fixed lights would fail the audit, so the lights are a travelling pool of six: bounce, four platform points at the nearest town (they jump at mid-leg, out of view of the chase camera), and the headlamp spot moved each frame.
- Band per town: Mango Tide A, Folded Gardens and Cedarbell Shrine B, Drowned Observatory and Monsoon Exchange C, Saltlight Terminus D; night is E. The band changes on arrival (the scene swaps while docked).
- `?look=original` keeps the 4.6 scenes exactly (same nodes, same post, tone mapping none); every GU47 node and uniform is gated on the band path.
- The HDRI budget line (3.6 MB at 1k) is exceeded by 0.43 MB with the three files the PRD names; no 2k files are used. Not raised; WP-38 / E-A may re-encode.
- LT-16 blends are 1.2 s in and 0.9 s out: the default chase rig has no lag, so a shorter blend moved the eye more than 1 m per frame on the departure shot.
- LT-17 uses National 2 Narrow (the E-J motorsport face) for the enamel signage and TT Commons Pro Mono for the departure board; no E-J theme is a railway theme, and HudKit is not used by this game.
- LT-09: the snow roofs keep the roof-draw count (the Observatory's roofs move out of `roofs-0..2`), and `?look=original` builds exactly today's roof meshes.
- The headlamp needs `setLight` to move; `moveLight` falls back to `setPosition` when the extension is not attached.

### Changes (commits 5660f56ea, a472cb78e, d967039c3)

- **Shadows, root cause:** the engine's default sun-shadow fit (the 4.2.0 fit) widens every cascade to the bounds of every caster. This route's merged facades, roofs, rails, sleepers and pylon beams span every town and leg, so each cascade covered the whole line and no shadow shape drew. The diagnostic run `g21p4-d1` showed it: shadows on added 635 depth draws and darkened the frame evenly, with no shapes compared with `?fx=noshadow`. The band look now sets `__A3D_SHADOW_FIT__ = "current"`, the 4.3.0 fit the ninja-runner port also uses. `?shadowfit=legacy` restores the old fit and `?look=original` never sets it. Cast shadows now draw: the canopy, buildings and tram throw shadow across the plaza and the long building shadows fall over the cobbles in the chase view.
- **Key:** bands A and B swing the key 30 deg (A 32 -> 62, B 38 -> 68) so it rakes the Mango Tide facades and throws shadow across the docked view. The key is 15% stronger; HDRI fill drops from 0.62/0.5 to 0.5/0.4 and the hemisphere from 0.24/0.22 to 0.2/0.19. Exposure goes to 1.4 (A) and 1.45 (B): r2 read 92.1 and 76.7, under the bands. The HDRI rotation follows the key as before.
- **Shadow settings:** maxDistance 130 -> 80, splitLambda 0.8, 2048 maps; contact shadows 0.5 -> 0.8 m. Vignette A/B 0.2/0.22 -> 0.28/0.3.
- **Band E:** exposure 2.0 -> 1.8. With the uniform old-fit darkening gone, r1 read 40.9, over the 28..40 band.
- **WP-36:** `lamplightTramEnamel` (Meshy retexture of `tram.glb`: cherry enamel, cream band, brass, teak, with metallic and roughness maps) was admitted with `aura3d assets import-meshy --profile vehicle`: `failures: []`, candidate quality.
