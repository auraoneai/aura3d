## Decisions

- Practicals: the C-35 audit counts every non-key light against `practicals` (max 6) and allows one shadowed light for racing. The WP's 26 fixed lights would fail the audit, so the lights are a travelling pool of six: bounce, four platform points at the nearest town (they jump at mid-leg, out of view of the chase camera), and the headlamp spot moved each frame.
- Band per town: Mango Tide A, Folded Gardens and Cedarbell Shrine B, Drowned Observatory and Monsoon Exchange C, Saltlight Terminus D; night is E. The band changes on arrival (the scene swaps while docked).
- `?look=original` keeps the 4.6 scenes exactly (same nodes, same post, tone mapping none); every GU47 node and uniform is gated on the band path.
- The HDRI budget line (3.6 MB at 1k) is exceeded by 0.43 MB with the three files the PRD names; no 2k files are used. Not raised; WP-38 / E-A may re-encode.
- The headlamp needs `setLight` to move; `moveLight` falls back to `setPosition` when the extension is not attached.
