## Phase-2 visual fix

Judged by eye against the 4.6.2 frames (`wp38/.../before/bunker-zero`) and the recap2 build: every floor was a washed-out beige, blue or pink haze. Cause, in three layers (all presentation, PRD D8):

1. `effects.volumetricFog` (the F6 moon shaft) was mounted on every boot. A mounted node replaces the scene fog colour and density in the forward program and adds the dominant spot's inscatter, so every floor got pale lamp-coloured haze (`f79215922`: mounted only on an F6 boot, inscatter 0.3, IBL fill 0.05-0.12 instead of 0.35).
2. Round 1 (`g11fix1`) showed the haze gone, but walls and floors were flat, evenly lit white. The CC0 colour maps do not show in the software WebGL2 capture, and the `#ece8df` base albedo read as white plaster. `629949a98`: wall, accent and floor base colours now come from the floor palette (the 4.6.2 tones, +0.1 / +0.08).
3. The F6 shaft drew no shaft on software WebGL2 and tinted the silo pink with the beacon's inscatter. `96fd99898`: the shaft is opt-in with `?godrays=1` (D11).
