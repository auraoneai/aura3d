## Decisions

- No hemisphere (WP: 0.25): the art direction declares IBL fill and the audit rejects a hemisphere; the IBL carries the fill.
- One shadowed light per floor (key spot, or the moon on F6) so `auditArtDirection` passes; the second shadowed spot waits for E-J's audit change (comment in lightRig.ts).
- F6 wall set is Concrete034 and the Kill House Tiles141: the PRD names six wall sets for seven slots.
- Reduced flash follows reduced motion or `?reducedFlash=1` (no separate setting exists in the game).
- Doors now slide sideways instead of sinking (BZ-15 acceptance "y constant"); the sim's `open` value is read only.
