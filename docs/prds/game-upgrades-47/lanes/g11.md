## Phase-2 visual fix

Judged by eye against the 4.6.2 frames (`wp38/.../before/bunker-zero`) and the recap2 build: every floor was a washed-out beige, blue or pink haze. Cause, in three layers (all presentation, PRD D8):

1. `effects.volumetricFog` (the F6 moon shaft) was mounted on every boot. A mounted node replaces the scene fog colour and density in the forward program and adds the dominant spot's inscatter, so every floor got pale lamp-coloured haze (`f79215922`: mounted only on an F6 boot, inscatter 0.3, IBL fill 0.05-0.12 instead of 0.35).
2. Round 1 (`g11fix1`) showed the haze gone, but walls and floors were flat, evenly lit white. The CC0 colour maps do not show in the software WebGL2 capture, and the `#ece8df` base albedo read as white plaster. `629949a98`: wall, accent and floor base colours now come from the floor palette (the 4.6.2 tones, +0.1 / +0.08).
3. The F6 shaft drew no shaft on software WebGL2 and tinted the silo pink with the beacon's inscatter. `96fd99898`: the shaft is opt-in with `?godrays=1` (D11).

## Phase 3 (4.7.1)

### What changed

- **Root cause of the flat greybox walls (every phase 1/2 frame):** the BZ-04 texture refs in `art/textures.ts` had no `kind: "aura-asset-ref"`. The primitive texture compiler (`compiler/textures.ts:75`) only resolves a URL when that tag is present, so none of the nine CC0 sets ever loaded. This was an app bug, not an engine limit; the phase-2 note blaming the engine is withdrawn.
- **Colour maps are now detail maps:** each map is resized to 512 px and scaled per channel in linear light to a mean of 0.8. Each material colour is the palette tone divided by its map's mean (`compensatedBase`, gain 0.85 on walls and floor, 1.4 on the ceiling). If a map has not finished uploading, the surface keeps the panel-approved phase-2 tone; in round g11p3-r1 that case showed a 2-3x wash. Metal046B (mean 0.05) is no longer mapped to a wall slot: F3 walls are now Metal063 with a Bricks075A accent, and the F5 accent is Concrete034.
- **Load order:** the boot floor is built first, so its maps upload first on a `?floor=` boot. Software WebGL2 presents one frame every 5-10 s, and in slot order floors 2-6 were still untextured at 60 s.
- **Ceiling:** the sealed ceilings now use the board-formed Concrete034 slab instead of a black void.
- **Post:** AO 0.9/1.2, contrast 1.2, vignette 0.42.
- **Light and surfaces:**
  - The carried lamp now reaches 14 m with decay 2 (was 32 m with decay 1.15, which lit every wall evenly).
  - Hostile rim is 0.6.
  - Floor roughness is 0.62, the floor map repeats twice per cell, and all surface maps use anisotropic filtering 8.

**Why texPending is still non-zero (measured, not fixed).** The e6 fallback removed the stall: every 60 s count is lower
than every phase-7 count. What is left is the throughput of the upgrade queue:

- **Every floor is built at boot.** `main.ts` builds all 7 floor slots (floors 1-6 and the Kill House) at boot, one slot
  per `slot * FLOOR_SPAN`. The boot slot is first in `buildOrder`, so its maps queue first. All 188 textured primitive
  resources go into the one queue.
- **Maps are decoded once per material, not once per URL.**
  - `upgradeProductionPrimitiveTextures` (`packages/engine/src/agent-api/compiler/textures.ts`) runs 4 resources at a
    time (`PRIMITIVE_TEXTURE_UPGRADE_CONCURRENCY`).
  - The `bitmapLoads` cache is per resource. Each resource fetches and decodes its own colour and normal map.
  - The game uses about 20 distinct texture URLs (the `bz-*` ambientCG sets and decals), so there are hundreds of
    decodes.
  - On the runner a stalled `createImageBitmap` costs 3 s (`PRIMITIVE_TEXTURE_DECODE_FALLBACK_MS`) before the `<img>`
    path starts.
- **The queue drains too slowly for the shot.** The 120 s wait before a shot is too short to drain 188 resources at
  that rate.
- **The off-screen floors (confirmed by the probe below).** Because the boot slot queues first, the rows still pending at the shot are
  the other slots and the decals. The frames show textured walls, floors and doors on the boot floor.

**Measured per row (probe `g11p7e6-probe1`, `tests/browser/gu47/g11-texprobe.spec.ts`, uncommitted, remote only).**
The probe booted `default` and `?floor=4` with no texture wait. Of the 188 rows, 122 read `none` (untextured

- **Order.** Four resources are in flight (stage `decode` / `decode-fallback` / `body` / `fetch`); the rest read
  `queued`. The queue lands about one resource every 30 s. The boot slot's walls, accent, floor and ceiling (f0 for
