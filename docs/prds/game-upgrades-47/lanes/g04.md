### Task 1: post-panel tunes `d0dc2b7d0` and `ae1b79d5e` reverted

- **Reverted:** `270d438bc` and `358c7edb4`.
- **Why:** neither tune was ever captured. Its stated reason, "an empty band array fails the mount", is false: `SkyBackgroundPass` always packs a fixed `u_bandParams[4]`, and the panel's own dusk and fog frames (recap2, no bands) render.
- **What the tune did:** it pushed night further towards the washed sodium-orange facades the judges criticised (window glow 1.6, exposure 1.3, contrast 1.25).
- **Replacement:** the phase-3 rounds below, each checked against the 4.6.2 frames (`wp38 before`) and the panel frames (`integration recap2`).

**Finding: the panel frames predate the 4.7.0 engine fixes.** Commits `16e85748f` and `b2edb52ed` (a volumetric-fog node no longer supplies the scene fog) and `3061427b0`/`acd2d27e0` (gradient skies draw) landed about 5 h after recap2.
- The panel's dusk "orange murk" and fog "white bank" came from the volumetric node acting as a pale exp2 scene fog.
- The released 4.7.0 build no longer draws either. r1 at the 4.7.0 values measured fog mean 72 and dusk without haze.
- So the shipped 4.7.0 dusk and fog views did not match what the panel judged. Phase 3 retunes towards the panel intent on the current engine.
