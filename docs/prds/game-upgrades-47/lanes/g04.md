### Why the cast shadows did not read

- **Probe (`p4-probe1`, spec `tests/browser/gu47/teleop-shadow-probe.spec.ts`):**
  - `diagnostics().lighting.shadows` shows the sun shadow rendered and sampled, with 3 cascades and 0 skipped. Casters include the instanced ones.
  - Contact shadows ran.
- **Finding:** shadows were on, but `?a3dLighting=shadows=off` moved the noon frame by only 1.2 luma (93.3 to 92.2).
- **Where they were:** the ×8 difference image (`p4-probe1/frames/noon-shadow-diff-x8.png`) shows them under the pod, the cable car and a tree, and along the left kerb. They were too faint to see.
- **Cause:** the fill is never shadowed, and the HDRI carries its own sun. The PRD key values (dusk 2.2, noon 3.0) were about 15% of the street's light.
  - Fit from rounds r0-r2: IBL contributes about 64 per unit of intensity at noon and about 36 at dusk.
  - Over the same rounds the key contributed about 12 at noon and about 11 at dusk.
  - Cutting the fill alone (r1: IBL 0.5/0.55; r2: 0.2) left the shadows invisible and dropped the frame (dusk mean 47, noon 53).
- **Fix: a key-dominant rig.**
  - IBL dusk 0.2 and noon 0.25; hemisphere 0.22.
  - Key dusk 5.5 and noon 7.5. The PRD angles (9 and 62 deg) and colours are kept.
  - Shadow maxDistance 90, split lambda 0.85, softness 0.3.
  - The filter changes from PCSS to PCF. Round 3 drew a checkerboard of coarse blocker texels round the pod. Part of that pattern is the road-tile patches and stays.
- **Handoff to the engine:** `resolveShadowSystemConfig` sets `normalBias = 1.5 × texelWorld` in metres, and `Prd02ShadowBridge` multiplies it by `texelWorld` again (its comment says the value is in texels).
- **Deviation from PRD GD-01:** the key intensities differ from the PRD row. `look.test.ts` now checks that the key is more than 10× the IBL, not the PRD numbers. The colours, angles, 2048 map and 3 cascades are kept; the filter is PCF, not PCSS.
