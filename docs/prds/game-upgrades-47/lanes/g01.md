## Phase-2 visual fix

Judged by eye against the 4.6.2 frame (`wp38/.../before/top-tennis`, default only) and the phase-2 frames (recap2, tune round 1).
Every number is software WebGL2.

Problems found and fixed (presentation only, gameplay untouched):

- Desert and beach haze: exp2 fog 0.011 / 0.01 put 18-22% fog on the court from the 45 m eye, and the desert volumetric
  stage only added extinction. Fog is now under 15% at 45 m on every venue and no venue runs the volumetric stage (d9f714958).
- Heavy DOF blur: cut-aways opened to f/2 at 120 mm (about 1 m sharp). They now use f/8, and the broadcast lens stays f/4,
  so both athletes stay sharp (d9f714958).
- Black speckle on hard/desert/beach courts and a black Moon court: roughness fields were bound as normal maps. Court grain
  is now a fabric normal. The London grass court drew white because of the vertex-coloured stripe mesh; the mow stripes are
  now two material-coloured meshes (7c7ec8b80).

  broadcast eye showed the rear seats GLB (fitted to 24 m) as giant chairs floating over the far stand, so it now shows
