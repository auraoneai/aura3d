# Aura3D Product Remediation PRD

**Baseline under remediation:** 1.5.0
**Status:** in progress
**Inventory artifact:** `tests/reports/aura3d-product-inventory.json` (regenerate with `node tools/product-remediation/build-product-inventory.mjs`)
**Interaction evidence:** `tests/reports/showcase-interaction-audit/` (regenerate with `npx playwright test tests/browser/showcase-route-interaction-audit.spec.ts`)

Release and marketing are frozen for the duration of this work. No npm publish, no
GitHub release, no marketing deploy, no version change, no route-status promotion.

---

## 1. Why the previous verification regime failed

Verification was screenshot composition: colour histograms, flat-region fractions
and foreground bounding boxes measured on a **first frame**. Every gate was green
while the product was visibly broken.

That regime is structurally incapable of detecting:

| Defect the user found | Why pixel metrics cannot see it |
| --- | --- |
| Focus control draws a flat yellow bar | A bar has the same colour histogram as a ring |
| Callout labels never render | Evidence counted label *nodes*, not drawn labels |
| Boxes floating beside the scene | A box in frame is a box in frame regardless of what it annotates |
| A button that changes nothing | No control was ever pressed |
| Car sinking through the road | The first frame is captured before motion |
| Attack damage without contact | Not visual at all |

The correcting change is that **runtime and interaction quality now define
success**. Composition metrics remain as supporting signals only.

## 4. Defects found by clicking, not by metrics

Recorded because they are the evidence that the audit methodology works:

1. Product Configurator: focus indicator rendered as a bar (library defect, fixed).
2. Product Configurator: callouts absent (library defect, fixed).
3. Digital Twin Ops: helper geometry floating away from the workcell (fixed).
4. Digital Twin Ops: `Focus Zone` did nothing but write a log line (fixed).
5. Digital Twin Ops: mode/zone state not published, so effects were unobservable (fixed).
6. Blockfall Reactor: pause/reset/replay unreachable on mobile (fixed).
7. Data Galaxy: three controls initially appeared inert; two were segmented options
   already active (audit methodology corrected to deselect first), one was a slider
   the harness was not driving past its step (harness corrected).
8. Turbo Drift Circuit: five momentary drive controls appeared inert because they
   bind `pointerdown`/`pointerup` rather than `click` (harness corrected to press,
   hold and release).

Items 7 and 8 were flaws in the audit, not in the routes. They are listed because
an audit that reports false defects is as useless as one that reports none, and
because the corrections are what make the remaining results trustworthy.

## 5. Remaining work

Tracked in the plan and not yet complete:

  vehicle dynamics and AI driving; Turbo migration.

- Phase 12: application kits for configurator, digital twin, architecture, smart
  city and cinematic routes.

  `hardcoded-helper-placement` findings, public API design rules.
- Phase 4: honest Three.js ecosystem parity measurement.
- Phase 15: clean-room developer proofs.
- Phase 17: full retained evidence including video and viewport variants.

## 6. Honest status

The four game routes remain `prototype-blocked` in
`tools/showcase-library/route-gates.json`. Their runtime simulation gaps -- vehicle
grounding and contact, opponent driving, jump trajectory and landing, combat
timing -- are not yet addressed and are not claimed to be.

The static and enterprise routes now have verified interaction behaviour. They do
not yet consume reusable application kits, so their route-local line counts remain
high.

Aura3D has **not** earned another release.
