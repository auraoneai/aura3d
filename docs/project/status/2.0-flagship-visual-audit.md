## Scope and method

This ledger records the exact four-route audit run after the Product Configurator
material-override, environment, and responsive-framing repair. It exists to
prevent a passing test, a nonblank canvas, or a contact sheet from being
misreported as visual parity.

The exact command was:

```bash
A3D_INTERACTION_ROUTE_IDS=showcase-product-configurator,showcase-smart-city-control,showcase-cinematic-architecture,showcase-digital-twin-ops \
  pnpm exec playwright test tests/browser/showcase-route-interaction-audit.spec.ts --reporter=line
```

Result: `4 passed` in 1.8 minutes. For every route, the reviewer opened the
full-resolution `final`, `interaction-final`, `desktop`, `tablet`, `phone`, and
six animation-frame PNGs at original detail. All 44 files were inspected, in
that order, for blank or fallback rendering, clipping, distortion, illegible
composition, responsive loss, interaction visibility, and temporal stability.
The contact sheets were navigation aids only; verdicts came from the original
PNGs. The JSON receipts are generated evidence and remain under
`tests/reports/showcase-interaction-audit/`.

This is an internal review by Codex during implementation. It does not satisfy
the PRD's independent-human-review gate. Any later source, asset, camera,
material, lighting, environment, responsive-layout, or capture change
invalidates the affected route verdict and requires a fresh run and inspection.

Smart City was refreshed separately after native instancing, distance LOD, and
runtime-culling, selected-building focus, and repeated-frame evidence landed at
source commit `b62fafb290eec4a311b8e44ab0fafc2ce10ebaa1`. The route-filtered
interaction audit passed 1/1 at `2026-08-10T07:51:20.575Z`: 12/12 discovered
controls passed, all 12 remained available on mobile, camera drag and restart

The reviewer reopened and inspected the complete regenerated 11-file Smart City

the after PNG against its interaction-audit receipt, takes the immutable
baseline's known-HUD and OCR regions, adds OCR regions from the current image,

changed dashboard cannot become scene-improvement evidence merely because its
