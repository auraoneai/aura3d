## Findings

1. **The 10-08 black frames do not reproduce on b60a669a.** Every capture that reached a screenshot has `blank=False`.
   Every `ready` capture has drawCalls > 0, including 01/10/11/12/16 under `all`. This is the opposite of pipeline
   2926601350 (01/10/11/12/14/16 ready with drawCalls 0). Code-read attribution of the fix (*(code-read)*): T0-15 MSAA
   resolve (#767), T0-16 state-cache invalidate (#768), T0-17 no per-frame throws (#780) and T0-01 (#408) are all on
   main.
2. **`post` alone is pixel-neutral on these scenes.** Every scene captured in both `none` and `post` has the same luma
   mean, and the PNGs for 01, 12 and 14 are byte-identical (`cmp`). The bench scenes author no post effects (`extra.bloom.enabled=false`), so A3D_QR_POST changes nothing visible
   here. No lane-03 regression is attributable in this run.
3. **`all` and `all,-post` match.** For 01, 10, 11, 12 and 16 the PNGs are byte-identical (`cmp`), and where both
   sets reached ready, drawCalls/luma match. Under both sets every scene logs `POSTPROCESS_V2_UNMIGRATED` ("legacy
   postprocess chain skipped under A3D_QR_CORE_OUTPUT"). This is by design: `Renderer.ts:695-699` (lane 01) drops the
   legacy chain under core output and records `output.postSkipped`, until lane 03 routes post through PostGraph v2 as a
   `post-hdr` contributor (#595, Q-03-2/Q-03-3). So with core output on, post does not run in either set, and
   removing `post` changes nothing. This is not a regression, and no `qr-ic-regression` was filed. The
   `all`-vs-`none` luma difference comes from other lanes (core output and lighting), not from post.
4. **No `POST_FIELD_UNSUPPORTED`** (`compiler/postprocess.ts:245-250`) in any scene, any set: 0 `payload.errors`, and
   no page error comes from the compiler.
5. **Harness gaps (filed #904, to:prd12).**
   - `ci.sh:48` joins sets with `,` and `bisect-summary.mjs:23` splits on `,`, so `all,-post` is lost
     (`missing: ["-post"]`) and the `all` rows are counted twice.
   - `capture.mjs` records no `diagnostics().post`, so per-scene post skips can't be listed.
6. **Not lane 03 (no lane-03 issue filed).**
   - `rgba32f ... must contain exactly 8388608 bytes` (`Texture.ts:447`, owner 06) fails 02/03/13 under `all`.
   - The 14-particles `all` page error (`TypeError ... reading 'length'`).
   - Readiness takes 87-118 s on every scene against a 120 s timeout. The timeouts move between sets
     (10/11 time out under `post` but are ready under `none`; 16 is the reverse), so the `none` control itself is
     flaky on this runner. That is T0-EXIT-1's ready ≤ 30 s target, not a post effect.
