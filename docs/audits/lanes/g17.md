# Lane G17: Serpentine Sky Run (`serpentine-sky-run-port`)

Branch `audit-fix/g17`, cut from `audit-fix/integration` (b2dba3713). Source: `apps/showcase-serpentine-sky-run-port` (a 3-line shim) and `examples/saiyan-snake-way-port` (the whole game). Evidence: `evidence/audit-fix/g17/` (`v437/`, `before/`, `calibration-e2/`, `after/` with canvas-only JPEGs and `meta.jsonl`; `shots-after/` with the full-page retake and its `capture-log.txt`).

Captures: remote software WebGL2 (SwiftShader), 1280x720, `examples/saiyan-snake-way-port/index.html?manual=1&autostart=1`, 360 idle steps (s = 108.8 m), shots 20 s and 50 s after stepping. The tables use the 50 s shot. "Lit surfaces" is the mean luma over the 30,567 pixels that change between `?env=0` and `?env=1` (the runner and the Meshy props), computed from the JPEGs.
