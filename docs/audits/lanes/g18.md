## Wave 2

| Handoff | From | Outcome | Commit | Evidence |
|---------|------|---------|--------|----------|
| `examples/drift-and-deliver-port`: the authored hemisphere now adds next to the environment; retune if the +10.7% lift is unwanted (108.21 to 119.81) | E2 | applied (retuned) | 040e2eb60 | see below |

**Verification of the handoff.** Correct. `look.ts` adds `lights.hemisphere({ name: "sky-fill", intensity: 1.7 })` next to `environments.hdri` (env-sky). Up to 4.5.0 the engine dropped that hemisphere (LIGHT-05), so the shipped and tuned HDRI frame never had it; the 1.7 only lit the lights-only `env=0` path (heal rung `no-env`). On the E2 branch, `?skyfill=1.7` reads 119.81 at 90 s, and `?skyfill=0` reads 108.21, identical to the base frame. So the whole +10.7% is the hemisphere, and it is unwanted: it lifts the shadowed street (bottom third 90.8 to 103.0) and flattens the sun contrast the game was tuned for.

**Change.** `SKY_FILL = { withEnv: 0.3, lightsOnly: 1.7 }` and `skyFillIntensity(env, override)` in `look.ts`: next to the HDRI the fill is 0.3 (a small directional sky/ground lift on top of the tuned frame), and the lights-only path keeps 1.7. New diagnostics switch `?skyfill=N` in `main.ts`. Unit: `tests/unit/drift-and-deliver-port/sky-fill.test.ts` (2 cases). Remote spec: `tests/browser/audit-fix/drift-and-deliver-port.spec.ts` (canvas-only luma + chunked JPEG, pattern of `e2-lighting.spec.ts`).
