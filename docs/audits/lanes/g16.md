### Calibration (remote, steady state, 60 s shot)

Method: `tests/browser/audit-fix/g16-chroma-light.spec.ts` loads `examples/splat-rush-port/index.html?manual=1` at 1440x900, drives the shotsb "roll" state (begin, 45 steps, `runAutopilot(420)`), freezes the sim and grabs the page at 0, 30 and 60 s. The 30 s and 60 s shots are identical, so the probe had settled. Calibration builds were temporary detached worktrees of `audit-fix/integration` and `audit-fix/e2` with `backdrop.ts` patched to read `?g16n=n<intensity>`; that patch was never committed. The GLB mask is every pixel where `n1` differs from the unmodified build by more than 10 luma (51.4k px, 4.0% of the frame: islands, towers, balloons, gems). The control region is every pixel no variant moves by 3 or more. Card = `evidence/splat-rush-port/screens/final/card@2x.jpg` (built from 0d965903c, before 4.5.0; the game sources differ from it only in `index.html` and `models.ts` URL plumbing).

| Variant | Base engine: GLB luma (% of card) | E2 engine: GLB luma (% of card) |
|---|---|---|
| shipped (no environment) | 106.9 (56.9%) | 100.4 (53.5%) |
| neutral 0.35 | 130.0 (69.2%) | 150.0 (79.8%) |
| neutral 0.5 | 148.0 (78.8%) | 165.2 (87.9%) |
| neutral 0.75 | 172.2 (91.7%) | 185.4 (98.7%) |
| neutral 1.0 | 191.0 (101.7%) | 201.1 (107.1%) |
| diffuse 0.5, specular 1 | 191.0 (101.7%) | 201.5 (107.3%) |
| **neutral 0.8 (committed, after run)** | **176.8 (94.1%)** | **188.4 (100.3%)** |

Card GLB luma 187.8. Control region within 0.4 luma in every variant. The diffuse/specular split row matches `neutral 1.0` exactly, so on this path the neutral probe uses the single knob and the split options do nothing; one `intensity` is the only lever.

Decision: 0.8, chosen for the merged engine (E2's LIGHT-05/08 lands in the same integration). On the base engine alone it reads 94.1% of the card, outside the audit's 5% target, and the target is reached only once E2 is merged. Whole-frame mean moves 188.06 to 191.40 (E2) because about 4% of the frame gets brighter. 21-32% of the frame is already pure white (P-01), and the GLBs do not add to it.
