## 05. Iron Crown (`iron-crown`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-iron-crown/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-iron-crown` · **Genre:** action

**State:** The game loads on production with no console errors and no failed requests (58 resources, 6 typed fighter assets, 176 draw calls). The probe ran in the Prism cloud browser on SwiftShader software GL. Steady-state mean luma was 56.0 to 56.3, with 14 to 15% of pixels below luma 12, 0% clipped, and the centre brighter than the border (77 to 82 vs 53). Quick Bout starts on its own. Keyboard movement and guard work, and the HUD clock and hit counters update. At 0.12 to 0.29 fps the probe never reached a round end. On desktop the game plays. On phones it is broken, because the touch Left/Right buttons do nothing (IC-01).

**4.5.0 impact:** The release changed only the version line in `apps/showcase-iron-crown/package.json` (`git diff --stat v4.3.7 v4.5.0 -- apps/showcase-iron-crown`: 1 file, 1+/1-), so every change reaches the game through the engine. Most of those engine changes leave it alone. Every stage look has either an HDRI or the explicit night-city neutral fill. No `lights.ambient` is authored. Bloom, vignette and contactShadows keep it on the HDR chain, and every primitive option is in `PRIMITIVE_OPTION_KEYS`. Three engine changes do alter the frames, and none of them has been captured on 4.5.0: contact shadows are now a real mask (`main.ts:239`), AO now reads real depth, and Neo-Tokyo is +6.4% brighter (31.4 to 33.4, `evidence/games450/iron-crown-neo-tokyo.md`). This needs a recapture, not code changes.

**Tests and evidence gaps**

- There are no unit tests for meter gating, the defense KO, ladder advance, round, time-up or draw logic, or touch bindings. The existing tests cover only faces, restyle, stage props and the quick bar (`tests/unit/iron-crown/`).
- The final screens date from 2026-10-25 (commit 55bc3de3a), before 4.5.0's real contact-shadow mask, real-depth AO and particle fix. `06-results` is "not reached" at both 1280 and 390, and the 390 mid frame still shows pre-fix floating gold orbs. Retake on 4.5.0 for Crown Arena and Neo-Tokyo, including a results frame, and record `diagnostics().drawCalls`.
- The Neo-Tokyo luma evidence relies on `/tmp/games450-b1-final/games450-capture.spec.ts`, which is not in the repo. `tests/browser/game-luma-capture.spec.ts` has no iron-crown target.
- The +6.4% Neo-Tokyo lift falls outside the CHANGELOG's "within about 5% inside the games" claim (`CHANGELOG.md:32`).
- Nobody has compared fused bloom (`separable`) against `?aura3d-render=production` for this game.
- Contact shadows on the spot-lit night stages modulate only the weak night key light (night-city key 0.7). No frame shows whether the 4.5.0 mask is visible here.
- The engine reports `fps 60` in diagnostics while the probe measured 0.12 to 0.29 fps of rAF. The warning that `step()` ran before the renderer mounted persists. This is an engine diagnostics issue, not a game-code issue.
- The page loads the shared 2.6 MB `aura-assets-BECYe7eh.js` catalog through `../../../../src/aura-assets` (`src/game/stages.ts:2`). Of the catalog URLs that were checked, 522 return 404.

**Dropped on verification**

- C12 "techChance dead code" was kept, but merged into IC-13 and held at P3. The probe's lopsided score comes from software GL and is not proof of an AI bug.
- The probe item "idle/crouch use Block1" was downgraded from P2 to P3 (IC-12). The stand-in is a deliberate, commented choice (`fighters.ts:109-110`) and is not broken.
- RP-04 (a URL-option typo stops the mount) is an engine-wide debug-switch robustness issue. It does not affect normal Iron Crown players, so it is not listed as a game defect.
