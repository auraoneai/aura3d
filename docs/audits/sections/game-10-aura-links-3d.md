## 10. Aura Links 3D (`aura-links-3d`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-aura-links-3d/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-aura-links-3d` · **Genre:** sports

**State:** Production loads cleanly. The live probe (Prism, SwiftShader software GL) saw `data-aura3d-ready=true`, 141 draw calls, no console errors and no failed requests out of 43 resources. Steady-state mean luma was 138.1 at both 123 s and 998 s, with 0.1% black pixels and 0% clipped. Aim, swing, out-of-bounds penalty and re-tee all work. The game cannot be finished in normal play. The swing logic presses power and accuracy for the player in every mode, and the auto power sends every putt about 50% past the cup, so no hole ends. The health rating is broken.

**4.5.0 impact:** The release changed only `package.json` in the game folder (`git diff --stat v4.3.7 v4.5.0 -- apps/showcase-aura-links-3d`: 1 file). Engine changes still alter every course. `effects.contactShadows` (main.ts:944-951) is now a real mask instead of a no-op. Real-depth AO comes in through the look presets. On Neon Bay (night-city) and Mare (space), the hemisphere light now counts in the ambient term, which grows it 2.29x and 4.09x (main.ts:920, 922). No Links frame has been captured since the release. The 4.5.0 luma check covered only the default boot (St Auroras hole 1, one frame at 480x300). CHANGELOG.md:32 claims "within about 5% inside the games", but docs/upgrade-4.5.0/aura-links-3d.md:53 says "this game was not measured". There is no evidence of a regression, and none that the courses are fine. Each course needs a capture.

**Tests and evidence gaps**

- There are no unit tests for createSwingMeter, stepBall cup capture and lip-out, putt sizing, advanceTour, the lab scoring or the camera modes. The 33 tests in tests/unit/aura-links-3d cover stroke-commit, posture, clubs, arm sweep and aim. This gap is why C1, C3, C4, C5, C9 and C10 shipped. Add a sim test that autoplays every hole of every course and requires each hole to be holed in 12 strokes or fewer.
- No evidence reaches a hole-out or the scorecard (evidence/aura-links-3d/screens/final/README.md). new-game-checklist.md:38 requires a results frame.
- No 4.3.7-vs-4.5.0 steady-state capture exists for any course (LIGHT-02, RH-03, RH-04). Capture all six courses with ?course=<id> at 1280x720 and 390x844 on the remote runner, starting with Neon Bay and Mare (ambient up 2.29x and 4.09x, LIGHT-03), and compare the golfer region as well as the mean luma.
- The final screens and the index poster date from 2026-10-25, before the 4.3.4 arm fix and before 4.5.0 (RH-05). Retake them after C1 is fixed.
- route-health.json claims "Immediate playable boot with smart autopilot" and "shot-shaping via accuracy/spin clicks" and lists the replaced ball-rack asset. It is still marked `unreviewed` (generatedAt 2026-10-17). game-inventory.json marks the game "playable". Downgrade both until a hole-out is captured (C16).
