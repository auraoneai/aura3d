## 07. Aura Hoops 3D (`aura-hoops-3d`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-aura-hoops-3d/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-aura-hoops-3d` · **Genre:** sports

**State:** The game loads and plays on production. In the Prism cloud browser (SwiftShader software GL, about 0.2-0.5 fps) it showed 0 console errors, 0 failed requests out of 65 resources, `data-aura3d-ready=true` and 326 draw calls. Mean luma was 80.7, 86.1 and 100.7 at t=52, 463 and 859 s, with 0% black and 0% clipped pixels. Keyboard input works: a held shot released, the CPU rebounded and dunked, and the check, live, pass, loose and check cycle ran. Desktop play is healthy. Touch play is broken because the touch controls are mounted inside a hidden container. The rules have several input and possession defects that make the shot meter trivial and dead balls go to the wrong team.

**4.5.0 impact:** The game source did not change (`git diff --stat v4.3.7 v4.5.0 -- apps/showcase-aura-hoops-3d` shows only `package.json`, 1 line). All 4.5.0 changes reach Hoops through the engine:
- `effects.contactShadows` (main.ts:783) is now a real mask on the shadow-casting key light that every look carries.
- The look's ambient occlusion now reads real depth.
- Shanghai (`night-city`) and Orbit (`neon-arcade`) get the explicit neutral fill.

The upgrade guide records remote steady-state luma: Shanghai 53.5 to 55.6 (+3.9%), Orbit 109.4 to 113.3 (+3.6%), Rucker 82.8 to about 88 (+6.3%) (`docs/upgrade-4.5.0/aura-hoops-3d.md`, section 2). The two night courts are inside the CHANGELOG's 5% target and Rucker is slightly outside it. Manila, Athens and the sixth court were not measured, no 4.5.0 frames are committed, and the contact mask was never inspected visually. The change looks acceptable, but the release has no receipt for these courts. Debug URL switches (`?aura3d-render=`, `?aura3d-bloom-fusion=`) now throw on a typo and stop the mount, which affects every game.

**Tests and evidence gaps**

- No tests cover the game rules in `main.ts`. `tests/unit/aura-hoops` has 5 files and 20 tests, all passing, covering assets, dribble, hit-stop, locomotion and shot physics. Nothing tests meter quality, input edges at 60/120/144 Hz, dead-ball possession, `mustClear`, scoring, the tour or mode switching, which is how C2-C6 and C13 shipped. `src/hitStop.ts` is dead code, imported nowhere.
- No browser check covers touch play under coarse-pointer emulation (C1). Every 390x844 capture ran with autopilot on.
- No results-screen frame exists, which checklist line 38 requires. `route-health.json` dates from 2026-10-17 and has no draw-call or triangle numbers (checklist line 40).
- The exposure-parity entry has identical before and after means (34.80530789686004), a 404 and a 500 console error, and only the Rucker court.
- The 4.5.0 upgrade guide's checklist is still open (release audit RH-04). The screens and posters predate 4.5.0 (RH-05).
- The live probe ran on SwiftShader at about 0.2-0.5 fps, so its fps and its "release — 0%" shot quality reflect frame starvation, not the game.

**Dropped on verification**

- C12's claim that "no committed number exists for Hoops Shanghai or Orbit after the final fill" is wrong. `docs/upgrade-4.5.0/aura-hoops-3d.md` section 2 records 55.6 and 113.3 on 4.5.0, so C12 is downgraded to P3 and limited to the unmeasured courts and the missing frames.
- The code audit's note that the touch layout uses the `fight` preset is wrong. The route uses `preset: "dpad-4btn"` (`main.ts:1199`). The bindings themselves are fine.
