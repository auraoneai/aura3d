## 06. Pip's Big Leap (`pips-big-leap`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-pips-big-leap/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-pips-big-leap` · **Genre:** platformer

**State:** The game loads on production with no console errors and no failed requests (65 resources, none >= 400). It reports `data-aura3d-ready=true` on the production runtime. In the Prism cloud browser (SwiftShader software GL, so 0.8 fps is not a real-GPU number) World 1 held a steady mean luma of 163.3-163.4 with 0% black and 0% clipped pixels, and 157.4 after moving. W moved Pip, Space jumped, the timer ran and an acorn was collected. The game plays, but one controller bug soft-locks it: touching any non-respawn hazard or the boss leaves Pip frozen in the `hurt` state for good. Strafing also runs Pip in a circle. Overall health is degraded.

**4.5.0 impact:** The release did not touch game code. `git diff --stat v4.3.7 v4.5.0 -- apps/showcase-pips-big-leap` lists only `package.json` (1 line). Every change comes from the engine. Contact shadows (world.ts:317, all 7 worlds) are now a real screen-space mask. Real-depth AO now reaches every look preset. The w6 `polar-night` look gains an explicit neutral fill at 0.85. Bloom fuses on `auto`. The measured luma shifts are moderate: w1 157.4 to 166.9 (+6.0%) and w6 108.1 to 111.1 (docs/upgrade-4.5.0/pips-big-leap.md:34). The final screens and posters date from 2026-10-25, before the release. The 4.5.0 frames were never recaptured or inspected for contact-shadow or AO artefacts, and bloom fusion was never diffed against the production chain. No regression is proven, but the release is unverified for this game.

**Tests and evidence gaps**

- No sim unit tests exist for player.ts, boss.ts, save.ts or the gate logic. The only dedicated spec is `tests/browser/shotsd-pips-big-leap.spec.ts`, a screenshot run, which is how C1-C3 shipped. All three reproduce in a few lines of node against player.ts.
- The final screens (2026-10-25) predate 4.5.0, and `meta.json` records "results: not reached", so there is no completion frame (RH-05).
- The upgrade guide's contact-shadow checks were never ticked: no `contactShadows.passExecuted` reading and no recapture of all 7 worlds (RH-04, C13).
- AO is on through the look preset in every world, and only the w1 and w6 mean luma were measured. No crops of the contact areas (feet, pickups on the ground) were checked (LIGHT-06).
- Bloom fusion `separable` was never diffed against `?aura3d-render=production` for this game (RP-05).
- The live `geometryCertification` failure (13.264 s traversal < 30 s minimum, `assetBoundLevel=false`, all `presentationCounts` 0) means the asset-bound certification is not met on the live build. Do not claim it.
- A typo in `?aura3d-render=` stops the mount (RP-04, engine-wide, P3).

**Dropped on verification**

- C13 (4.5.0 contact-shadow regression, P2): the change was measured for this game (w1 +6.0%, w6 108.1 to 111.1 luma, docs/upgrade-4.5.0/pips-big-leap.md:34), and the live frame is not dark or clipped. No regression is shown, so the item was merged into the evidence gaps above.
- C12 (test gap) was merged into "Tests and evidence gaps" rather than listed as a defect.
- Live-probe jumpsLeft 1 to 0 on first landing: this affects the evidence snapshot only, with no player effect.
