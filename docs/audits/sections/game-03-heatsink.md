## 03. Thermal Drift (`heatsink`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-heatsink/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-heatsink` · **Genre:** racing

**State:** The game loads and renders on production. A Prism cloud browser run (SwiftShader software GL) logged 0 console errors and 0 failed requests out of 72 resources. Mean luma was 87.5 at t≈136 s and 92.8 after about 20 s of racing, with 0.1% dark and 0% clipped pixels. Space (fire) and Shift (afterburner) both respond, and the lap timer and sectors advance. As a game it is incomplete. The race never ends, the player cannot take damage or lose, the race position is cosmetic, and phones get no controls. Health: degraded.

**4.5.0 impact:** The release changed only `package.json` in this app (`git diff --stat v4.3.7 v4.5.0 -- apps/showcase-heatsink`: 1 file, 1 line). The authored-lights-only change and the no-HDRI neutral fill do not apply, because `looks.preset("outdoor-day")` (main.ts:600) brings an HDRI. Two engine changes do reach the game. The look's ambient-occlusion pass now reads real depth, and bloom now runs through the fused 'separable' path. No frame of this game has been captured on 4.5.0, so the size of either change is unknown. The authored hemisphere light (main.ts:614, 0.88) is still dropped next to the HDRI environment, as it was on 4.3.7.

**Tests and evidence gaps**

- The game has no unit or replay tests. `tests/unit/heatsink-port/*` covers Ion Circuit (examples/heatsink-port), not this app. The sim lives inside the `onFrame` closure of a 2,013-line main.ts and cannot be imported.
- The final screens date from commit d658eb2a1 (2026-10-25). The game has no capture on 4.5.0 (RH-05), no 4.3.7-vs-4.5.0 steady-state luma (LIGHT-02), no AO on/off pair (LIGHT-06), and no fused-vs-production bloom diff (RP-05).
- The exposure-parity reading (evidence/exposure-parity/frames/scenes.json, 22.904 before and after) came from a 9 s boot. The final-screen ready times were 41-99 s, so that frame was probably the loading overlay and does not count as evidence.
- The 4.5.0 draw-call figure (1121) comes from SwiftShader, where measured rAF was 0.24 fps. No real-GPU frame time exists.

**Dropped on verification**

- Probe "aria status reads 'POS 4//12'": the source does not support it. The chip is `P` + `#h-pos` + `#h-posof` ("/12") (index.html:867), and main.ts:1882 writes a single "/". No source adds a second slash.
- Probe "diagnostics fps 60 vs measured 0.24": this is an engine diagnostics and SwiftShader artifact, not a defect in this game. It is noted under evidence gaps only.
- heatsink-C18 and heatsink-C19 were merged into the evidence gaps above.
