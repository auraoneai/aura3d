## 01. Aura Slam 3D (`top-tennis`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-top-tennis/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-top-tennis` · **Genre:** sports

**State:** The production page loads with no console errors and no failed requests. `__AURA3D_ROUTE_READY__.status` is `ready`, all 3 typed assets are `ready`, and the backend is WebGL2 with 119 draw calls. It drops straight into a match. The probe ran on SwiftShader software GL. Mean luma was 52.5 (49.3 mid-rally), 7.9% of pixels were below luma 12 and none clipped, so the frame is lit and not uniform. Serve, fault and second serve all worked. A full point and the results screen were not reached at 0.4-0.85 fps. The game is playable on desktop. It has visible defects: a white crowd, wrong key hints, and no athletes in view on portrait phones.

**4.5.0 impact:** The game source did not change. `git diff --stat v4.3.7 HEAD -- examples/aura-slam apps/showcase-top-tennis` shows only the version bump in `package.json`. All changes come from the engine:
- The scene has bloom, vignette and colorGrade, so it stays on the HDR chain. Under quality `auto` it gets separable bloom fusion and the cascade skip.
- It has 24 instanced spectators, below the 1,024 dense-instancing threshold, and no OPTION_IGNORED triggers.
- The scene authors both ambient and hemisphere lights (`view/lighting.ts:19-20`), and 4.5.0 now counts the hemisphere. The computed ambient term rises from 0.492 to 0.861 (x1.75).
- The only measurement is the exposure-parity row: 60.9 to 61.4 (+1%), taken on `/examples/aura-slam/?heal=0` at 320x200 after a 9 s boot. That does not match the computed 1.75x, and no steady-state capture of the live route exists.
- The fused bloom was never compared against the production chain for this game.

The release looks safe here but is unverified. `docs/upgrade-4.5.0/top-tennis.md:3-5` says nothing was built, run or measured.

**Tests and evidence gaps**
- The game's evidence and screens are dated 2026-10-25 (`git log -1 --format=%cs -- evidence/aura-slam`), before 4.5.0. The results screen (06) was never captured at 1280 or 390.
- No 4.3.7-vs-4.5.0 steady-state luma capture exists for the live route, and no fused-bloom-vs-production diff exists (`?capture=1` vs `?capture=1&aura3d-render=production`).
- None of the 31 files in `tests/unit/aura-slam` covers spectator colours (C1), refresh-rate independence of the adapter (C4), serve-position legality (C5) or portrait framing (C2).
- The live probe could not finish a point at software-GL speed. The scoring, reset and game-over paths need a QA-hook run (`startOffline` + `stepFrames`) on the remote runner.
- `info().render.triangles` reported 0 alongside 119 draw calls. The cause was not traced, so do not use this counter as evidence until it is checked.
