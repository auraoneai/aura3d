## 09. Aura FC 3D (`aura-fc-3d`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-aura-fc-3d/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-aura-fc-3d` · **Genre:** sports

**State:** Production loads and Quick Play works. The live probe ran on SwiftShader software GL: `data-aura3d-ready=true`, 379 draw calls, no console errors, no failed requests (48 resources). Holding KeyD moved `h-fw` 3.08 m and play went from kickoff to live. Steady-state mean luma was 111.4 at t=114 s and 114.9 at t=594 s, with 0% black and at most 0.1% clipped, so the frame is never blank. Frame rate was about 0.15-0.3 fps on software GL, which says nothing about real GPUs. The boot path is healthy. Under ordinary play the sim has real defects: the Touch Lab freezes, shootouts are one-sided, and a slide tackle on a keeper loops every frame. The goal net is drawn at about 4x size, and phone touch controls cannot move up or down.

**4.5.0 impact:** For this app the release changed only `package.json` (4.3.7 to 4.5.0). The game uses look `interior-industrial` (an HDRI plus a shadowed directional key light), 4 authored spot lights, and `effects.bloom`, `motionBlur` and `contactShadows` (`src/game/scene.ts:390-392`). It therefore stays on the HDR post chain, and the "authored lights only" and no-HDRI fill changes do not apply to it. Four 4.5.0 engine changes alter its frames: contactShadows is now a real screen-space mask, motion blur and the look's ambient occlusion now read real depth (AO read zero depth on 4.3.7), and bloom uses separable fusion by default. Nobody has captured FC since 4.5.0. The final screens date from 2026-10-25, and FC has no 4.3.7-vs-4.5.0 luma or AO comparison. The live luma looks sane, but the size of the shift is unmeasured.

**Tests and evidence gaps**

- FC has had no capture since 4.5.0, which changed four effects the game uses: contactShadows, motion-blur depth, AO real depth and bloom fusion. The final screens date from 2026-10-25. The release audits LIGHT-02, LIGHT-06, RH-04, RH-05 and RP-05 all name FC.
- No 4.3.7-vs-4.5.0 steady-state luma result exists. FC is missing from `tests/browser/game-luma-capture.spec.ts` TARGETS, and no AO-on/AO-off pair has been made.
- `tests/unit/aura-fc-3d/` covers only the rig, ball physics, gait, kicks, locomotion and pass flight. No test covers the shootout (FC-02, FC-03), the Touch Lab (FC-01), a tackle on a keeper (FC-04), extra time, pause, circuit progression, the HUD or Favela goals.
- No recorded frame shows a goal mouth with the net at its real size, a half-time or results screen, or the portrait camera with players in view.
- Debug URL switches `?aura3d-render=` and `?aura3d-bloom-fusion=` reject the mount on a typo (RP-04, all games). This is a P3 shared engine issue, not specific to FC.

**Dropped on verification**

- None of the code-audit findings was refuted. C4 (code audit) and the live probe's slide-latch observation describe the same defect and are merged into FC-04. The live probe's note that two defenders started a slide in the same frame has no mechanism behind it yet, so it is not listed.
