## 08. Aura Diamond 3D (`aura-diamond-3d`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-aura-diamond-3d/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-aura-diamond-3d` · **Genre:** sports

**State:** The page loads on production (`data-aura3d-ready=true`, 71 resources, none failed, all 41 `/aura-assets` URLs return 200) and boots into Quick Play in Harbor Sandlot. The Prism probe ran on SwiftShader software GL. It measured steady-state mean luma 86.2 to 88.2, 0% black, 0% clipped, 658 to 662 draw calls, 0.08 to 0.21 fps and a JS heap of 305 to 423 MB. During the first session the tab went to about:blank on its own after about 650 s; the cause is not proven. The game renders correctly, but manual play cannot finish a match in four of six parks because every human swing is forced into a perfect home run (C1). Health: broken for manual play, fine for rendering.

**4.5.0 impact:** The game's own source changed by one line between v4.3.7 and v4.5.0 (the `package.json` version), so every 4.5.0 change here comes from the engine. The scene has post effects, transparent materials and particles, so it stays on the HDR chain. The no-HDRI looks used by Midtown (night-city) and Lunar (polar-night) now get the neutral 0.85 fill; the upgrade notes report Skyline mean luma 71.0 to 74.5. `effects.contactShadows` (`main.ts:979`) went from a no-op to a real screen-space mask. Real-depth AO also changes every look-preset game. Neither change has been captured for this game: the final screens are dated 2026-10-25 and the 4.5.0 visual-regression run checked assertions only. Nothing looks broken live, but the release needs a recapture before it can be called verified here.

**Tests and evidence gaps**

- Only `contact.ts` and `batter-pose.ts` have unit tests (`tests/unit/aura-diamond/`). Count, outs, runners, scoring, mercy and the bracket live inside the `boot()` closure in `main.ts` and cannot be tested, which is how C1, C2, C6 and C7 shipped. Move that logic into a pure module and test strikeout, walk, two-out sac fly, walk-off, mercy and bracket advancement.
- The final screens are dated 2026-10-25, before 4.5.0, and `evidence/aura-diamond-3d/screens/final/README.md` says results screens were not reached. Recapture on 4.5.0 at 1280x720 and 390x844 in all six parks, including a results screen and AO/contact-shadow on-off pairs (RH-05, LIGHT-06).
- The 4.5.0 visual-regression run checked `diamond-live-432` assertions only, with no pixel diff (`evidence/visual-regression-4.5.0/prd2.md:92-94`).
- `route-health.json` has no draw-call or triangle record.
- No motion-gate result exists for this game.
- Real-time batting could not be tested live: the Prism probe ran at about 0.1 fps on SwiftShader, so a pitch takes minutes of wall time. The live input result (Space held, called strike) is a frame-rate artefact, not evidence against C1.
