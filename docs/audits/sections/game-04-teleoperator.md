## 04. Grid Dispatch (`teleoperator`)

**Live:** https://aura3d.auraone.ai/apps/showcase-teleoperator/ · **Source:** `apps/showcase-teleoperator` · **Genre:** racing

**State:** The game loads and renders on production. The live probe (Prism, SwiftShader software GL) saw no console errors, no failed requests out of 60 resources, and a bright daytime frame: mean luma 106-108, 0-0.7% dark pixels, 0% clipped, about 97 coarse colours. Keyboard input works: holding W raised speed from 11.3 to 12.9 m/s. Nothing is P0. The real problems are in the game itself. The fare loop needs no skill, the car is drawn on the centre line, phones get no controls, and two camera modes sit inside most of the cars.

**4.5.0 impact:** The scene uses `looks.preset("outdoor-day")`, which has an HDRI, plus authored ambient, hemisphere, directional and point lights, bloom, vignette and colorGrade. That keeps it on the HDR post chain and the explicit-environment lighting branch, so the "authored lights only", no-HDRI fill and direct-path changes do not apply. The changes that do apply are separable bloom fusion, real-depth AO from the look preset, and the cascade skip. None of them has been measured on this game. The last frames were taken on 2026-10-25, before the 4.5.0 engine. The authored hemisphere light (`src/main.ts:560-565`) is still silently dropped, as it was on 4.3.7. No primitive option triggers OPTION_IGNORED. Health on 4.5.0 is unproven but nothing is visibly broken.

**Tests and evidence gaps**

- No test covers the ride state machine, scoring or the per-car camera offsets. The only teleoperator unit tests are the collision and likeness ones (C21).
- There is no frame from the 4.5.0 engine. The final screens date from 2026-10-25. The results screen was never reached (`meta.json`: "results: not reached"). Ready shows 240 s, "never lit" (RH-05, C21).
- No 4.3.7-vs-4.5.0 steady-state luma pair exists, and no capture compares AO on and off. The game is not in `tests/browser/game-luma-capture.spec.ts` TARGETS (LIGHT-02, LIGHT-06).
- Bloom fusion has not been diffed against `?aura3d-render=production` (RP-05).
- `docs/upgrade-4.5.0/teleoperator.md` checks are still open (RH-04).
- No 390x844 touch spec, and no camera-mode sweep across the five cars.

- C22 (enhancement list) was merged into C7, C1, C19 and C3. It adds no separate defect.
