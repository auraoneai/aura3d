# Task 13: Coral reef underwater with camera orbit

Build a single-page browser app with the provided framework, served by vite. It must render one full-viewport canvas with id #app. The scene is a shallow tropical coral reef seen underwater. Show a sandy seabed with rippled patterns, several coral formations in different shapes (branching, rounded brain, fan or tube shapes) and colours, and swaying seaweed or anemones. A school of at least 30 small fish should swim in a loose group, plus 2 or more larger solitary fish on their own paths. Sunlight should enter from above, with a bluish-green water tint, depth-based colour fade, and drifting caustic-like light patterns on the sand. A few bubbles or particles should rise slowly. The camera orbits slowly around the reef centre at a gentle height, with a slight bob, and no user input is needed. Do not rely on external downloads; build everything from simple procedural shapes.
