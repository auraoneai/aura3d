# Task 9: procedural robot with idle animation under dramatic lighting

Build a scene with {{LIBRARY}}.

Requirements:
1. A robot character built procedurally from primitives (no model files): head, torso, two arms and two legs, with at least five articulated joints (for example neck, shoulders, elbows, hips).
2. An idle animation driven from code: it breathes and shifts its weight, and its head and arms move gently. Frames must differ over time.
3. Dramatic lighting: a strong key light, a visible coloured rim light, and a dark background, with shadows on a floor.
4. The camera frames the whole robot, centred.
