# Task 12: Marble and gold trophy on a pedestal

Framework: {{LIBRARY}}.

Build a single-page browser app with the provided framework, served by vite, drawing into the existing canvas with id #app. Show an awards-ceremony trophy displayed on a stepped pedestal inside a dark, warm gallery space. The pedestal is white Carrara-style marble with grey veining, generated procedurally in code, for example with layered noise or turbulence-warped stripes, and with a polished, slightly glossy finish that faintly reflects the room. The trophy stands on top and is made of polished gold: a cup on a stem with two handles, built from lathe, revolved profiles or smooth curves, with a small engraved-looking band or ring in a darker, rougher gold, and a smaller base of black polished stone or dark wood with a thin gold plate. Gold must look like real gold: warm yellow, fully metallic, low roughness, with strong, slightly tinted environment reflections, not flat yellow. Light with one warm spotlight from above and in front that casts a soft pool of light and a visible falloff on the marble and a soft shadow behind the pedestal, plus a faint cool fill and a subtle rim light. Add a procedurally generated environment (a dark room with a few bright windows or lamps) so the gold has something to reflect. The camera makes a slow orbit of about 15 degrees each way around the trophy, keeping it centred and in frame. Expose window.__TASK_STATE__ = { materials: ['marble', 'gold', 'darkstone'], cameraAzimuthDeg: <current number> } updated every frame. All geometry, textures and environments must be generated in code; no external assets.
