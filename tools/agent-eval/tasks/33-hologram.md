# Task 33: hologram shield

Build a scene with {{LIBRARY}}.

Requirements:
1. A sphere drawn with a custom GLSL shader (not a stock material): a fresnel rim glow in cyan, horizontal scanlines that scroll upward over time, and an overall translucent look.
2. A small solid metallic cube sits inside the sphere and rotates.
3. A dark background with a floor that faintly reflects or shows the glow.
4. The scanlines move over time, so frames differ. The camera frames the sphere.
