# Task 1: rotating PBR cube with orbit control

Build a scene with {{LIBRARY}}.

Requirements:
1. One cube with a physically based (PBR) material: visibly metallic and rough (not a flat unlit colour).
2. Lighting that makes the cube clearly readable (key light plus fill or environment), on a dark-grey background.
3. The cube rotates continuously by itself.
4. The user can drag with the mouse to orbit the camera around the cube (orbit control), including while it rotates.
5. Camera framing shows the whole cube centred.
