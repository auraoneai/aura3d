# Task 31: a small orrery

Build a scene with {{LIBRARY}}.

Requirements:
1. A glowing star at the centre and five planets of different sizes and colours orbiting it at different speeds on different radii.
2. One planet has a ring around it.
3. A dark space background with a soft glow (bloom) on the star.
4. The planets move continuously, so frames differ over time. The camera looks down at an angle and frames all orbits.
