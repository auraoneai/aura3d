# Task 10: Fireworks display over a night harbor

Build a single-page browser app with the provided framework, served by vite, rendering to the canvas with id #app. The scene is a fireworks display at night over a calm harbor. Show a dark star-dusted night sky, a water surface that faintly reflects the light of the bursts, and a low dark silhouette of a shoreline or pier with a few small buildings or boats. Rockets launch from the shoreline one after another at irregular intervals, rise with a short glowing trail, and burst into spherical shells of many glowing sparks. Use at least three clearly different burst colors (for example gold, red, and blue or green), and make each burst fade and fall under gravity over a second or two before it disappears. Some bursts should leave trailing streaks. The sky and water should be lit subtly by the active bursts. The scene must animate continuously without user input, with a slow gentle camera drift or a fixed camera framing the sky and harbor. Aim for a polished, cinematic look: strong glow on the sparks against a dark background, no flat or washed-out colors, no visible geometry artifacts. Keep it smooth at interactive frame rates. Also expose window.__TASK_STATE__ = { bursts: <total number of bursts that have exploded so far>, activeParticles: <current number of live spark particles> } and update it every frame.
