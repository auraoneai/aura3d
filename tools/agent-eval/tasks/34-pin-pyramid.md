# Task 34: knock down a pyramid

Build a scene with {{LIBRARY}}.

Requirements:
1. A pyramid of 15 boxes (5 on the bottom row, then 4, 3, 2, 1) resting on a floor, with real rigid-body physics.
2. A ball launched automatically 1.5 seconds after load toward the pyramid at speed; after the crash the boxes tumble and settle under physics.
3. Pressing Space resets and relaunches the shot.
4. Shadows, a lit scene, and a camera that frames the pyramid and the ball's path.
