# Task 12: Procedural stylised tree (seasonal)

Build a single-page browser app with the provided framework, served by vite, rendering to the canvas with id #app. Model one large, characterful stylised tree entirely from code (no external model, texture or image files), as a hero object on a small grassy mound. It must have: a thick trunk that flares into visible surface roots at the base and tapers upward, at least 3 levels of branching (trunk, major limbs, twigs) with at least 12 branches in total that fan out in different directions and taper, and a foliage crown made of at least 15 separate leaf clumps of varied size and tone (at least 3 distinct greens or autumn colours) hanging on the branch ends. Add a few details: a knot or hollow on the trunk, a small number of fallen leaves or a couple of mushrooms on the ground, and a rope swing or a bird house hanging from a limb. The branch layout must be generated by a seeded procedural routine (same seed gives the same tree). The camera frames the entire tree with sky behind and slowly orbits. Use a key light with shadows so the crown casts a shadow on the mound. After the first frame set window.__TASK_STATE__ = { ready: true, branchCount: <branches>, foliageClumpCount: <clumps>, seed: <number> }.
