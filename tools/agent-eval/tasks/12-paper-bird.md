# Task 12: Flapping paper bird with looping flight

Build a single-page browser app with the provided framework, served by vite, rendering to the canvas with id #app. Show a stylized bird made only from simple geometric shapes: a body, a head with a beak and two eyes, a tail fan of at least 3 feathers, and two wings each made of at least 2 hinged segments (an inner and an outer part). The bird flies in a smooth circular path of about 6 world-units radius at constant height, banking into the turn and always facing its direction of travel. The wings flap continuously at roughly 2 flaps per second, with the outer segments lagging slightly behind the inner ones so the wing bends. The body should rise and fall subtly with each flap. The loop should repeat every 8 seconds with no visible jump. Place the bird over a simple ground disc with a few static props (for example cones as trees) for scale, and a soft sky-colored background with one directional light and shadows. Use a chase-style camera that stays outside the circle and looks at the bird so that it stays in view. Do not load external model files.
