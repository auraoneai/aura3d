# Task 8: stylized low-poly island with water and a day/night sky

Build a scene with {{LIBRARY}}.

Requirements:
1. A stylized low-poly island (flat-shaded faceted terrain, a few trees or rocks on it) built procedurally from code. No model files.
2. A water surface around the island that is clearly visible and animated.
3. A sky that changes with time: a day/night cycle that moves the sun and shifts the sky colours (a full cycle in about 20 seconds is fine).
4. Lighting follows the sun, and the scene is never black.
5. The camera sits above the water and frames the whole island; it may orbit slowly.
