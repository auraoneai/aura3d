# Task 36: patrolling soldier

Build a scene with {{LIBRARY}}.

Requirements:
1. The provided model `/assets/soldier.glb` (a skinned soldier with clips `Idle`, `Walk` and `Run`) is placed in the scene and plays the `Walk` clip in a loop.
2. The soldier walks along a circular path of radius 4 around the origin, always facing the direction of travel, so it moves in the world, not in place.
3. Press `r` to switch to `Run` and move faster along the same path, `w` to switch back to `Walk`, with a smooth crossfade.
4. A ground, lights with shadows, and a camera that follows or frames the circle so the soldier stays visible.

The rendering contract in the next section applies.
