# Task 11: Pendulum wall clock with escapement

Build a single-page browser app with the provided framework, served by vite, rendering to the canvas with id #app. Show a freestanding grandfather-style pendulum clock built only from simple geometric shapes: a tall wooden case, a round clock face with 12 hour marks, hour, minute and second hands, and a swinging pendulum with a weighted bob behind a glass-like opening. The pendulum must swing back and forth with a period of 2 seconds (one full left-right cycle), with eased motion that is slowest at the extremes. The second hand must advance in one tick per second, and the minute and hour hands must show the real current local time. Also add two small hanging weights that sink very slowly. Use warm interior lighting and a simple floor and wall behind the clock. Front-facing camera, slightly above center, with the whole clock visible. Do not load external model files.
