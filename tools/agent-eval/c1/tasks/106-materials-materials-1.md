# Task 10: Glass, brushed metal and fabric spheres under studio light

Framework: {{LIBRARY}}.

Build a single-page browser app with the provided framework, served by vite, drawing into the existing canvas with id #app. Show a studio product shot of three spheres of equal size in a row on a seamless, softly lit, light-grey floor that curves up into the backdrop. Left sphere: clear glass (transparent, with refraction or at least a convincing tint, bright specular highlights and visible reflections of the studio lights). Centre sphere: brushed metal such as steel or aluminium (strongly metallic, with a stretched or blurred reflection that is clearly different from the glass). Right sphere: woven fabric such as dark red velvet or wool (fully rough, no mirror reflections, soft sheen or fuzzy rim light, a fine procedural weave or noise pattern visible up close). Light the scene like a photography studio: one large soft key light, a dimmer fill from the opposite side and a rim light from behind, plus an environment or reflection source so the metal and glass have something to reflect. The spheres cast soft contact shadows on the floor. The camera is fixed at a slightly raised, three-quarter-front angle with all three spheres fully in frame and centred. The three spheres must be recognisable as three different materials at a glance. Add a slow animation of the key light drifting side to side so the highlights move across the surfaces. Keep it running continuously. Expose window.__TASK_STATE__ = { spheres: [{ name: 'glass' }, { name: 'metal' }, { name: 'fabric' }], keyLightX: <current number> } updated every frame. Use no external assets: all geometry, textures and environments must be generated in code.
