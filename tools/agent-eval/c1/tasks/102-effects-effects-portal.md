# Task 11: Magic portal with swirling particles and glow

Build a single-page browser app with the provided framework, served by vite, rendering to the canvas with id #app. The scene is a magic portal standing in an ancient stone ruin at dusk. Build a tall ring portal (a standing circular frame of weathered stone or runic metal) on a stone platform, with moody dusk lighting and a few ruined pillars around it. The disc inside the ring is a swirling, glowing energy surface, such as a rotating vortex in cyan and violet with a bright core, animated continuously. Glowing particles spiral around and into the portal, and a few motes rise from the platform and drift upward, fading out. The portal casts colored light onto the nearby stones and platform, and the light should pulse slowly. Add a subtle slow orbiting camera or gentle camera sway so depth is apparent. Aim for a polished, atmospheric look: strong bloom-like glow around the portal, high contrast against a darker surround, a coherent color palette, and soft haze. Keep it smooth at interactive frame rates. Also expose window.__TASK_STATE__ = { portalActive: true, particleCount: <number of live particles>, time: <elapsed seconds> } and update it every frame.
