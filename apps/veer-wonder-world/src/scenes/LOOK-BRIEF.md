# Wonder World look brief

The look-dev target for `looks.ts` and the six world files (PRD 02 section 4; `.claude/skills/aura3d-art-direction`).

1. **Time of day.** Soft late-morning daylight in the four outdoor worlds, a bright high sky in the learning cloud, and a warm lamp-lit afternoon in the home nook.
2. **Palette.** Pastel grounds and skies (mint meadow, sky blue, butter cream) behind rich, saturated subjects: purple Momo, mint Lulu, warm yellow Lumi and Peeko, red GoGo, a red ball. A subject always stands out from the ground behind it.
3. **Mood.** Calm, cosy and easy to read: a soft key with gentle fill, modest grounded shadows, no glare, no flashing, no vignette, nothing dark or scary.

Frames are judged with `tests/browser/wonder-world-look.spec.ts` (remote software WebGL2, desktop 1024x768 and phone 390x844) against the rubric in `.claude/skills/aura3d-art-direction/references/quality-bar.md`. The rounds and their numbers are recorded in `evidence/veer-wonder-world/p1/look-dev.md`.
