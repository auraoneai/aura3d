HOW TO PLAY

Roll as far as you can: every wall has three holes, so steer into the one that matches your current shape.

Controls

Keyboard

  • ←→ADSteer left / right
  • SpaceEnterStart, or play again after a splat
  • PEscPause / resume
  • MSound on / off
  • CShape symbols on / off

Touch & mouse

  • DragDrag left / right anywhere to steer
  • TapTap PLAY NOW (or the screen) to start
  • ⏸︎Pause button, bottom left
  • 🔊Sound button, bottom left
  • AgainPLAY AGAIN restarts after a splat

Walls & holes

  • Sphere hole: fits a red sphere
  • Cube hole: fits a yellow cube
  • Cone hole: fits a blue cone
  • Wrong hole: OUCH, you lose a life (a shield blocks it). The rim that fits you glows; your shape is shown top right.

Pickups (roll through the orb)

  • Morph (colour-cycling): switches you to a different shape
  • Any shape (purple): every hole fits for 5s
  • Shield (teal): blocks your next miss
  • x2 score (orange): double points for 7s

Scoring

  • Fit through a hole: 40 × multiplier. Dead centre is a PERFECT: 60 × multiplier.
  • Every 3 fits in a row add +1 to the multiplier, up to x5. A miss resets it to x1.
  • Every 750 points: +100 milestone bonus.
  • Each fit grows your ball and adds speed; the road keeps getting faster.

Lives & the end of a run

  • You start with 3 lives (red dots, top left).
  • Each miss costs one life, shrinks your ball and breaks your combo.
  • Lose the last one and it's SPLAT! The run ends; beat your best score.
