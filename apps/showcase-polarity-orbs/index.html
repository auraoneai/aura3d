HOW TO PLAY

Roll down the track and hit the orb that matches your colour in every row: flip between red + and blue − to match, build a combo and survive as long as you can.

Controls

Keyboard

  • ←→ADSteer left / right across the three lanes
  • SpaceEnterFlip polarity (red ↔ blue), no cooldown; on the title and results screens they start a run or play again
  • PEscPause / resume
  • MSound on / off

Touch & mouse

  • DragSlide sideways to steer
  • TapTap or click without moving to flip polarity
  • PLAY NOWStart a run, or PLAY AGAIN after one
  • II♪Pause and sound buttons

On the track

  • +−Rows of three orbs. Each row counts the orb nearest to you, so you always hit one: your colour scores, the other colour costs a life.
  • −A row that is all one colour is always the colour you are not when it appears: flip before you reach it.
  • ★Rainbow (gold gem): for 5 s every orb turns gold and counts as your colour.
  • ◆Shield (mint gem): blocks your next wrong-colour orb, no life lost.
  • 2x2 (gold gem): double points for 7 s.
  • ◎Magnet (purple gem): for 6 s nearby orbs of your colour drift into your lane.
  • Gems float between rows: steer through one to grab it. The HUD badges show what is active.

Scoring

  • +20Each matching orb is worth 20 × your multiplier (doubled under x2). Your orb grows and the track speeds up.
  • x1–x5Every 3 matches in a row raises the multiplier by one, up to x5. The gold bar under your score fills towards the next step.
  • +100Milestone bonus every 750 points.

Lives & the end of a run

  • You start with 3 lives. A wrong-colour orb takes one, resets your combo to x1 and shrinks your orb. Lose all three and the run ends.

Options

  • +Polarity symbols on the title screen shows a + or − over your orb as well as its colour.
  • Reduced motion calms camera shake and flashes.
