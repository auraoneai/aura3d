Simulation ports, Aura3D 4.1.3

Game mechanics rebuilt on Aura3D

Eleven independent Aura3D reconstructions of browser-game mechanics. Each one replays a recorded reference of the original's simulation and states its limits on its card; the art is original look-alike work, not copied, and none is a pixel-parity or 1:1 port: in a blind test a model judge could still tell every port from its original. Not affiliated with the original games.

Strange Orbit — Aura3D portMoon-rim bike racing: a seven-lap cup race against five rivals with route splits, checkpoints and a rescue budget, rebuilt from a recorded run of the original. Simulation parity verified against the original's recorded seven-lap run; lit sci-fi look with audio and a difficulty selector; bikes, riders and rim props are generated models (riders are single rigid meshes); no career or garage. Splat Rush — Aura3D portColour-gate ball runner with power-ups, colour switching and a splatter game-over; every simulation field matches the original's recorded run. Audio, effects and keyboard play added; backdrop islands and pickups are generated models; platform SDKs are not ported; a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original. Serpentine Sky Run — Aura3D portEndless runner on a serpentine sky road: eight stages with boost rungs, roll sections and traffic hazards; the outbound run matches a recorded reference. Outbound run only (no hell run or road home); title, pause, results and audio included; the runner, gate and hazards are generated models; a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original. Drift & Deliver — Aura3D portArcade drift driving through a block city: coin pickups, 84 traffic cars, bloom and a chase camera; all four recorded scenarios reproduce exactly. Headlight beam renders; the player car, traffic and street furniture are generated models (pedestrians are static); collision response untested; a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original. HEATSINK — Aura3D portAnti-gravity time trial on the Vesper course with boost pads, pickups, barrels and heat management; the state replay matches the original within 1e-6. Simulation port only: the drone, pylons and scenery are generated models and the art is still visibly different from the original (a blind model judge picked the original in 35 of 48 trials and rated 40 of 48 pairs as the same game design); Time Trial only, no AI or audio. Ninja Runner — Aura3D portLane-running combat with twelve skinned clones, form changes and a full course win; the recorded 106-second run replays event for event. four generated rigged characters plus building and prop models; enemies share two bodies; sun shadows are off; a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original. Last Train to the Sea — Aura3D portTrain-driving loop with day and night, passengers, fares and tips; loop length and score match the original's recorded loop and memory stays flat over three loops. No garage or level select; the tram, townhouses, landmarks and passengers are generated models (passengers stand rather than sit); a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original. Roadster Hours — Aura3D portCoastal drive with 92 vehicles in one batched mesh; the recorded 48-second run replays exactly. Coastal look with sky, ocean and a lit road; vehicles and scenery are generated models and there are no shadows; a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original. Where the Wind Wanders — Aura3D portMeadow walk collecting three letters and ringing a bell; the walking trajectory matches the original within 1e-9 over 2,098 steps. Includes the boat epilogue, journal and audio; the traveller, cottages and trees are generated models; a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original. Silt & Signal — Aura3D portFree-ride flying over a silt world; three recorded rides reproduce the original's simulation state to 1e-9. Chapter 0 has real colliders; the rider, ring beacons and landmarks are generated models; later chapters are generated hills, with no NPCs or minimap; a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original. Cloud Runner — Aura3D portObstacle runner on a cloud: the first chapter's run model replays the original's recorded run to 1e-12. Includes creatures, boulders, the duel and finale, with audio; the hero, creatures and scenery are generated models; a blind model judge could still tell the port from the original.
