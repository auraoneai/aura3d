# Vault Breakers — Aura3D prototype

Neon pinball under the vault lights: fire motorised-joint flippers, bank five
target stands, crack the vault, ride the multiball. Three balls, one table, no
mercy. This is a **`prototype`** development showcase for the Aura3D root safe
API — not a production game, not a reusable pinball kit.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| `A` / `D` (or `←`/`→`) | Left / right flipper |
| Hold `Space`, release | Plunger charge → serve |
| `S` | Nudge (3 strikes tilt-lock the flippers) |
| `R` | Full reset |
| `P` / `Esc` | Pause |

Touch: hold the flipper / plunge buttons in the side panel.

## What this route proves (and what it does not)

Proven here, with evidence:

- **Player-actuated motorised joints.** Both flippers are `motorised-hinge`
  constraints driven by input at gameplay rate: they snap to their up limits in
  under 150 ms, hold as walls under ball load, and launch resting balls
  up-slope. See `SPIKE-FLIPPER.md` for the spike report and tuning history.
- **In-world extruded 3D text.** The back panel carries the live score as a
  physical score reel of `text3D` digit nodes (uppercase alphanumeric catalog);
  the DOM panel only mirrors it.
- **Deterministic route-local physics.** Fixed-step table, FNV-1a pose hashes
  at serve, once-per-entry sensor arming, and a full-reset state proof.
- **Authored pinball systems, labeled as such.** Playfield slope (a gravity
  component), nudge, bumper/slingshot kicks, the vault-door swing, and the
  shooter-lane auto-plunge are authored logic — not simulated spin, fluid, or
  mechanisms. No ball-spin or angular-ball claims.

Not claimed: a reusable pinball/game kit, ball spin physics, production
rendering, PBR parity, HDR/IBL, or WebGPU. Labels stay `prototype` until
independent human visual review approves the exact final artifacts; the route
is held out of the public showcase index until then (see `route-health.json`).

## Known engine limits (library tasks filed)

- **Opposite-sign simultaneous motors degrade in the vendored Rapier adapter.**
  Two actively rotating motorised hinges with motor speeds of opposite sign
  collapse the first motor. This route mirrors the right flipper's joint axis
  (`[0, -1, 0]`, negated limits) so both motors share one sign — see
  `SPIKE-FLIPPER.md`. Engine-level fix tracked as a library task.
- The adapter's velocity motor uses a fixed factor of 1, so flipper snappiness
  comes from the velocity target (60 rad/s) and light 0.18 kg bats rather than
  the torque ceiling.
- The bottom drain gap is narrower than the ball; the drain catch is a
  gap-region rule (a ball through the flippers has drained).

## Assets

All assets are original CC0 synthesized in-repo and registered through the CLI:

- Models: `vaultBreakersTable`, `vaultBreakersFlipper`, `vaultBreakersBall`,
  `vaultBreakersVaultDoor` (`scripts/build-models.mjs`).

  buses (`scripts/build-sfx.mjs`), unlocked by gesture.

## Claim boundary

Aura3D prototype: route-local pinball on the public `physics` surface with
motorised flipper joints (same-sign axis-mirror workaround). Rapier is the sole
physics owner. No `three` imports, no raw asset URLs, no string asset ids, and
DOM is UI only — every gameplay truth is rendered by the Aura3D scene.
