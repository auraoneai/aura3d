HOW TO PLAY

Roll your paint ball down the 1,200 m road and paint as much of it as you can before your tank runs dry.

Controls

KEYBOARD

  • ←→ or AD steer
  • SpaceEnter start / play again
  • PEsc pause / resume
  • M sound on / off

TOUCH & MOUSE

  • Drag left / right anywhere to steer (about 50 px per lane)
  • Tap PLAY NOW / PLAY AGAIN
  • II top-right pauses
  • ♫ top-right mutes

On the road

  • Your ball paints the lane it rolls over (6 lanes). The trail colour changes every 25 m.
  • Paint can — roll over it in its lane for +35 paint in your tank.
  • Solvent strip (one lane, 10 m) — −20 paint, washes that strip clean, nothing paints while you are on it, and your combo resets.
  • Eraser bot slides side to side — a bump costs −15 paint, wipes the last 15 m of road and resets your combo.
  • Finish gate at 1,200 m — reach it to complete the run.

Scoring

  • Every metre you paint scores 5 × your multiplier.
  • The road comes in 25 m stretches. Paint 80% of one for a STRETCH!: combo +1 and the road speeds up a little. Miss and the combo drops to x1.
  • Every 2 stretches in a row adds +1 to the multiplier, up to x5 (the gold tube fills towards the next one).
  • Every 750 points earns a +100 bonus.

Tank & the end of a run

  • One tank, no lives. It starts full and drains slowly as you roll; the bar turns red below 25%.
  • Empty tank = OUT OF PAINT, run over. Reach the finish = RUN COMPLETE. Your grade is your coverage (the share of road you rolled over that stays painted): S 85%+, A 70%+, B 50%+, else C.

Esc, Enter or Space to close
