HOW TO PLAY

Roll down the 1,200 m sunlit causeway and coat as much turf in glossy ink as you can before your tank runs dry.

controls

keyboard

  • ←→ or AD steer
  • SpaceEnter start / play again
  • PEsc pause / resume
  • M sound on / off

touch & mouse

  • Drag left / right anywhere or use the bottom lane pads to steer
  • Tap play now / play again
  • Top-right pause button pauses the run
  • Top-right speaker button toggles audio

on the road

  • Paint roller coats the lane it rolls over (6 lanes). Pigment hue cycles every 25 m stretch.
  • Paint canister — roll over it in its lane for +35 ink in your tank.
  • Solvent strip (one lane, 10 m) — −20 ink, washes that strip clean, pauses painting while on it, and resets your combo.
  • Eraser bot slides side to side — a clash costs −15 ink, scrubs the last 15 m of road, and resets your combo.
  • Finish gate at 1,200 m — reach it to complete the run.

scoring & grades

  • Every metre you paint scores 5 × your multiplier.
  • Paint 80% of a 25 m stretch for a STRETCH!: combo +1 and speed climbs. Miss and combo resets to x1.
  • Every 2 stretches in a row adds +1 to the multiplier, up to x5.
  • Every 750 points awards a +100 milestone bonus.

ink tank & coverage rank

  • One pressurized tank, no extra lives. It drains steadily as you roll and flashes red at 25% or below.
  • Empty tank = OUT OF PAINT. Reach 1,200 m = RUN COMPLETE. Territory grade: S 85%+, A 70%+, B 50%+, else C.

Esc, Enter or Space to close
