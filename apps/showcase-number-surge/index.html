HOW TO PLAY

Steer your number ball through the better gate in every pair to grow its number, then smash each wall by being bigger than it.

Controls

Keyboard

  • ←→ADsteer left / right
  • SpaceEnterstart a run, or play again
  • PEscpause / resume
  • Msound on / off

Touch & mouse

  • Dragdrag left / right anywhere to steer
  • PLAY NOWstart a run, or PLAY AGAIN after one
  • PAUSEpause button, bottom right
  • SOUNDsound button, bottom right

On the road

10Your ball shows your number. You start at 10; it never drops below 1.
+20Cyan gates (+N, ×2) grow your number. Every pair has at least one.
÷2Magenta gates (−N, ÷2) shrink it. You always pass one gate of each pair.
40Walls every 300 m show a gold number. Be bigger to smash through; the wall's number comes off yours.
Gold orb: +25 to your number, ×2 your number, or ×2 score for 7 s.
Green orb: a shield that blocks one wall you are too small for.

Scoring

  • A good pick (the better of the two gates) scores what it added × your multiplier.
  • Every 3 good picks in a row lifts the multiplier, up to ×5. The bar under your score fills as you go; a worse pick resets it to ×1.
  • Smash = wall number × 10 × multiplier. ×2 SCORE doubles everything.
  • +100 bonus every 750 points.

Lives & the end of a run

  • One life. Reach a wall with a number that is not bigger than it and the run is over, unless a shield blocks it.
  • Walls roughly double each time and the road speeds up, so keep picking the bigger gate.
  • P / Esc pauses, M mutes, Space or PLAY AGAIN restarts.
