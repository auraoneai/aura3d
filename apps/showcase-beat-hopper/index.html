HOW TO PLAY

Skate down three neon lanes on a 132 BPM beat: jump, big-jump, duck or switch lanes past every row, grab crystal beat orbs and score as much as you can before your 3 lives run out.

controls

Keyboard

  • ←→ADChange lane, one lane per press
  • Space↑Jump: quick hop over hurdles and trenches
  • ShiftWEBig / Boost Jump: higher 2.35 m arc & extended hangtime for safe clearance
  • ↓SHold to duck (or mid-air slam-duck), let go to stand up
  • EnterStart a run, or play again (Space works too on those screens)
  • PEscMPause / resume, or toggle sound

Touch, mouse & HUD pills

  • Swipe ↔Change lane: drag left or right across the runway
  • Tap / ClickJump immediately on tap, or click JUMP
  • BIG JUMP2× TapBoost Jump: click BIG JUMP, right-click, or double-tap
  • Swipe ↓DUCKDuck: swipe down or hold the DUCK pill
  • PAUSESOUNDPause and sound buttons, bottom right

on the track

  • Bass amp hurdle: dual-woofer stage cabinet. Hop over it, or step into an open lane
  • Laser trench: a deep chasm in the runway. Hop over it
  • Plasma gate: overhead emitter beam. Duck under it
  • Line-array monolith: tall speaker tower, too high to hop. Switch to a free lane (DODGE)
  • Beat disc: be in its lane as it passes to collect it
  • 32Every 32 beats the track gets faster and rows come closer together

scoring

  • 60PERFECT 60 / GOOD 30 × your multiplier: hop so you peak right over the crate or trench, duck just before the bar
  • +20DODGE 20 for passing a wall in a free lane, orb 15, both × your multiplier
  • x5Each PERFECT, GOOD, DODGE or orb adds to your combo. Every 4 in a row raises the multiplier by one, up to ×5. A MISS resets it to ×1
  • +100Bonus +100 every 750 points

lives & the end of a run

  • Hitting an obstacle in your lane (the wrong move, or a hop or duck far off the beat) is a MISS: you lose a life and your combo. Passing a hop or duck row in an open lane is safe but scores nothing. Lose all 3 lives and the set is over.

Esc, Enter or Space to close
