A small field guide

Find your rhythm.

Follow a rival to recharge boost. Take the marked side lane for a fast shortcut. Bike touring rewards smooth lines and slipstreams; skating rewards carving, loops and tricks.

Jump, then tap jump again in skate mode to kickflip. Clean landings earn boost and a speed surge. Enter mint loops with momentum; gravity can hold you on or rescue you after release.

Release acceleration to stop on the road. In space, steer left/right, hold Jump to rise, or Brake to descend. Touch down on the top of any lane to rejoin without spending gravity.

Watch the edge warning. If you escape into space, use gravity before your rescue window closes. A charge returns every lap. Explorer gives the most room to recover.

Finish races to unlock suits and vehicle setups. At the seafoam fork, steer left to climb or right to dive. At the opal split, take the right lane for the lower bypass, or jump across the platforms. Time trial saves your fastest run as a ghost on this device.
