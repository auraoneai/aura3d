Capabilities and proof

Every capability names its public path and the evidence behind it.

Typed browser 3D workflow

Create Vite apps, sculpt multi-material procedural assemblies or register GLB/glTF assets, import generated TypeScript references, compose scenes, test routes, and deploy static output.

Open reference

One renderer, two backends

WebGPU and WebGL2 from the same scene code, with PBR, image-based lighting, shadows, post-processing, particles, sky, water and weather as scene nodes. Rendering can move to a worker with one option.

Open reference

Assets and AI generation

Search, convert, optimize, validate and admit models with one CLI. Generate lifelike, rigged models and text-to-motion clips on fal.ai. Every asset is typed, hashed and licensed, and game assets are certified for scale, geometry and composition before they ship.

Open reference

The game stack

Game lifecycle, HUD, touch controls, juice, audio cue banks, a fixed-step loop, seeded replay, QA hooks, Rapier physics and genre kits. Every game in the showcase runs on them, and ?autoplay=1 plays any game through to its results screen.

Open reference

Every claim ships with a capture

Rendering capabilities are proven on the public route that uses them: route health, a judged capture and typed provenance back every PBR, HDR, post-processing, WebGPU, skinning and morph claim.

Open reference

Labs

Data Galaxy, Particle Lab and the two game-layer harnesses are engineering labs: they exercise the engine in depth and are listed separately from the product examples.
