Ship browser 3D in 18 lines of TypeScript.

Aura3D 4.0.1 (@aura3d/engine@4.0.1) is the open-source (MIT), zero-dependency TypeScript 3D engine that replaces ~120 lines of manual Three.js boilerplate with a unified WebGPU compute + WebGL2 runtime, compile-time typed GLB/glTF assets, physical PBR materials, HDR post-processing, Rapier physics, Recast crowd steering, Gaussian splats, portal worlds, and automated Playwright deployment verification.

Why Teams Switch from Raw Three.js

18 lines of declarative TypeScript instead of ~120 lines of fragile boilerplate.

Raw Three.js leaves you hand-wiring Draco/KTX2 WASM workers, PMREM environment generators, EffectComposer passes, Rapier/Recast glue, and recursive GPU memory disposal. Aura3D 4.0.1 unifies the entire application stack into one zero-dependency SDK.
