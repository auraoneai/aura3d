The Bellkeeper's Walk

Walk the meadow, collect three letters and ring the bell above the valley.

Play The Bellkeeper's WalkHow it works

Framing the bell twice

A six-second crane shot introduces the valley. Ringing the bell triggers a rising orbit around it. Reduced motion turns both sequences into cuts.
