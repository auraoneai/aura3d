Aura Slam 3D

Serve on grass, rally on clay, then take your game to the Moon.

Play Aura Slam 3DHow it works

What changes after the bounce?

Aura Slam hands the flight of the ball to Aura3D’s racket-sports kit. The game supplies the court surface and decides whether a shot clipped the line.
