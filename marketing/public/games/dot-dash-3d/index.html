Glint Courier

Sweep six glowing circuit boards for data shards while four sentries close in.

Play Glint CourierHow it works

An entire maze in a text grid

Each board starts as a 19-by-21 character grid. Symbols place the player, cells, cache and sentry house, and a separate palette gives the board its neon traces.
