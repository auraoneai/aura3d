Splat Turf

Paint your way along six lanes. Keep the tank topped up and protect your trail from eraser bots.

Play Splat TurfHow it works

Scoring the trail you leave

The game divides the course into cells and counts how many passed cells still hold paint. Erasing a painted cell can lower the final coverage grade.
