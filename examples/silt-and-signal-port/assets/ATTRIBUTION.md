# Asset attribution (CC0)

All surface texture assets in `./assets/textures` are CC0 from ambientCG (`aura.library.json`), and all 3D models (the articulated desert skiff surfer rig, hover-skiff, ruins, gatehouses, viaducts, mesa spires, lamps, and oasis flora, replacing earlier Kenney and generated GLB stand-ins) are authored procedurally with `@aura3d/engine` (`art/proceduralModels.ts`). Since the Idol wow pass the most-seen of them are drawn as Idol GLBs generated through fal from our own reference images (files and `*.idol.json` provenance in `assets/idol/`).
