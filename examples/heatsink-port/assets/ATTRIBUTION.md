# Ion Circuit (`heatsink-port`): asset attribution

None of these assets come from the original game.

All 3D hover-drones (`drone2`, `ionSentryDrone`, `ionHeavyDrone`), hazard barrels, energy/shield/repair pickups, race gate pylons, bioluminescent flora (`plant-cap`, `plant-coral`), and canyon monolith/arch ruins are authored 100% procedurally in TypeScript on `@aura3d/engine` (`art/proceduralModels.ts` and `scene.ts`). Zero Meshy or external `.glb` files are bundled or fetched.
