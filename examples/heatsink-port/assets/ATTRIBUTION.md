# Ion Circuit (`heatsink-port`): asset attribution

None of these assets come from the original game.

All 3D hover-drones (`drone2`, `ionSentryDrone`, `ionHeavyDrone`), hazard barrels, energy/shield/repair pickups, race gate pylons, bioluminescent flora (`plant-cap`, `plant-coral`), and canyon monolith/arch ruins are authored procedurally in TypeScript on `@aura3d/engine` (`art/proceduralModels.ts` and `scene.ts`). The most-seen of them are drawn as Idol GLBs generated through fal from our own reference images (`assets/idol/*.glb`, per-asset provenance in `assets/idol/*.idol.json`); the procedural builders remain as the fallback kit.
