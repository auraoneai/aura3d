A FEW BASICS. ENDLESS POSSIBILITIES.

FIND YOUR RHYTHM.

WASD / ARROWSMove to the ball. Hold left/right to aim; up for deep, down for short. Aim during the first 0.18 seconds of a swing.
SPACEMove the service target with arrows/WASD. Space tosses; press Space again as the rising ball reaches the ivory circle. This starts your swing and locks aim.
SPACESwing for a return. Your stroke adapts to the ball.
DOUBLE SPACEDouble strike within 0.22 seconds adds power and 0.5 m depth on serve, without energy cost. Rally power needs 38 energy.
RPress R to swing a high, deep lob. Hold left/right to aim; depth is automatic and power is ignored.
Q / EPrepare a short / deep shot. Up/down override this choice during the swing.
Shift + SpaceHold the power modifier and swing. Double pressing your swing button adds the same power.
CHold for controlled placement on normal or deep drives in Ace and Legend.
P / FPause / fullscreen.

PS4 controller: Left stick or D-pad: move and aim. Cross: toss or hit; press twice quickly for power. Hold R2 and press Cross for power. Square: short shot. Circle: deep shot. Triangle: lob in Ace or Legend. Options: pause or online options.

Xbox controller: Left stick or D-pad: move and aim. A: toss or hit; press twice quickly for power. Hold RT and press A for power. X: short shot. B: deep shot. Y: lob in Ace or Legend. Menu / Start: pause or online options.

Connect your controller, then press a controller button.

On mobile, drag the joystick or court to move; before serving, dragging moves only the target. HIT tosses; tap HIT again as the rising ball reaches the ivory circle to swing and lock aim. Double HIT within 0.22 seconds adds power, or tap POWER HIT. Releasing a drag never swings. In Ace and Legend, LOB prepares a lob.

First to 3 games, win by 2. At 3–3, play a tiebreak to 7 points, win by 2.

Ace / Legend shot choices: Drop-shot skill varies by player: Alcatraz 10/10, Zinner 9/10. Other players have lower ratings. Deep shots close to the net (inside 4.8 m) go out. Use a neutral or short shot there.

Build a point in Ace and Legend: Deep and wide shots can force a slower recovery and a weaker reply. Watch the opponent: attack the open court, change direction behind its movement, or follow deep pressure with a drop. Lob when it commits to the net. Vary your patterns as it learns repeated placement. Legend recovers faster and punishes predictable play; expect smaller openings against elite players.
