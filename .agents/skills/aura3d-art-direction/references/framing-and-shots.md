# Framing and shots

How to compose the frame so the look can be judged. A great look in a bad
shot still reads as a bad scene.

## Frame coverage

| Shot | Subject % of height | Camera | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hero/product | 50–70 | `orbit` or `perspective` fov 35–45 | silhouette + material, no crop |
| environment establish | 10–20 subject | `establishing`/`dolly` fov 50 | fog band, horizon, palette sweep |
| gameplay | 15–30 | genre rig | playfield + next obstacle in view |
| character | 60–70 full body / 40 head | `follow` side-on or `orbit` | gait, rim, contact shadow |
| night scene | 20–35 | `follow` street level | sign glow, wet reflections, pools |

## Composition rules that change the read

- Place the subject on a third line, not dead center, except for product
  symmetry shots.
- Keep the horizon near the lower third in outdoors — centering it flattens
  depth and starves the fog band.
- Give moving subjects look-ahead room (leading room) toward their direction.
- One accent hue per frame: the eye finds it; two accents fight.
- Fog and atmosphere only pay off at depth — an empty mid-ground shows
  nothing, so stage a second layer (trees, walls, props) between subject and
  horizon.
- Vignette/post grade darkens corners; keep key information out of the outer
  10% of frame.

## Shot checklist before capture

- [ ] subject visible at opening frame, not mid-spawn or mid-fade
- [ ] `mid` shot shows the busiest expected state (particles, opponents)
- [ ] `action` shot catches motion with the rim/key still readable
- [ ] background layer is at least one atmosphere depth behind subject
- [ ] HUD (DOM) frames the composition, not overlaps the subject

## Shot vocabulary

- `establishing`: wide, horizon in frame — palette and atmosphere carry it.
- `push-in`: slow dolly toward subject; shadow density deepens as you close.
- `tracking`: lateral follow; parallax shows depth layers + fog banding.
- `crane`: vertical reveal; fog gradient reads strongest here.
- `close-up`: material contrast + rim do all the work; fog irrelevant.
- `silhouette`: subject against the brightest part of frame — grade defines it.
