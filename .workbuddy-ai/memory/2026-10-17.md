## Arcade Slate 01 — three games on Aura3D

### Brief
Wrote `docs/prompts/arcade-slate-01-brief.md` (later superseded by `arcade/BRIEF.md`), rewriting
Gurbaksh's "reverse engineer these 3 games and rebuild with all Aura3D, rename, slightly different"
into an executable build brief.

Reference titles identified:
- `aymeric.dev/roadster` — Roadster Stunt Arena (3D stunt driving, thrusters, wall riding, time attack)
- `roadster.rocks` — Roadster Flip (one-button trick chainer, tap/tap-in-air/hold, flow multiplier)
- `miaai-lab.github.io/.../049-brick-party.html` — Brick Party (neon brick-breaker)

Two of three are "fan-made, not affiliated with Tesla", so "slightly different" was the weak point.
Replaced with an originality spec: mechanics borrowed, expression original (no Tesla mark, no
Roadster silhouette, no reference art/audio/code).

Renamed slate: **Vector Nine** (stunt arena), **Backspin** (one-button trick chainer),
**Prism Break** (circular brick-breaker). Each gets a real mechanical twist, not a reskin:
shared boost/grip energy pool; narrowing landing cone; ball shard split that shrinks the paddle arc.

### Decisions
- Apps live in `arcade/<game>/`, standalone, not in the monorepo's `apps/` route system. They alias
  `@aura3d/engine` to the repo's prebuilt `dist/` — no install step, no registry copy.
- `arcade/BRIEF.md` is the shared conventions file for all three games.
- `arcade/tools/shrink-glb.mjs` — welds, iteratively simplifies to the vertex ceiling, resizes
  textures to 1024, re-encodes. Raw Meshy output is 15-75 MB; guardrail is 20k verts / 2 MB.
- `arcade/tools/capture.mjs` — game-agnostic Playwright capture (opening/mid/action) + node dump.

### Prism Break — built and running
4 Meshy assets generated (paddle, core, brick, pylon), decimated to ~15k verts / <1 MB each,
admitted through `assets import-meshy` with rights evidence. 120 credits.

Bugs found and fixed:
1. **TDZ**: `createGame` invokes the scene factory immediately, so state the scene builder reads
   must be declared *above* the call. Symptom: `Cannot access 'bricks' before initialization`.
2. **`app.camera.use()` takes a rig, not a spec.** The scene `.camera()` slot alone did not drive
   presentation under `createGame`; symptom was a black canvas with 29 draw calls.
3. **`primitives.torus` is authored at radius 0.43 and scaled by `size`** — pass `R/0.43`, not `R`.
4. **Rim tube scales with size** (~0.35 at arena scale), so the paddle orbited *inside* the torus
   and was occluded by the far rim. Moved paddle to r=2.70, y=0.46, inside the glow band.
5. Rim emissive at 0.9 bloomed to pure white and swallowed the frame — dropped to 0.32.
6. `primitives.plane` is XY; rotate `[-PI/2,0,0]`. Size must be scalar.

State at capture: 71 bricks, 25-29 draw calls (bricks are one instanced node), split working
(34° → 22° arc, 3 shards), no console errors.

### Parallel agents — did not work on this machine
Spawned two background agents (Backspin, Vector Nine). Both generated their Meshy assets
(Backspin: hoverbike, roofgate, roofmodule, skylinetower; Vector Nine: barrier, mast, tower —
missing its car) but the box could not sustain two concurrent vite builds: file-descriptor
exhaustion, `resource temporarily unavailable: /dev/null`, broker socket ECONNREFUSED, builds
stalling 12+ minutes, and eventually the shell itself failing on `echo`. Both agents were stopped
after ~30 minutes. **Run heavy builds serially on this machine.** Credits: 1232 left.

### Open defect — RESOLVED
**Root cause: a tilted perspective camera clips everything beyond its target plane.**

With `camera.perspective({ position: [0,7.4,5], target: [0,0,0] })`, every render item whose world
bounds sat past the target was silently clipped — the entire far half of the arena. Bricks, paddle
and ball all vanished; primitives with large bounds (the rim, the floor) survived, which made it
look like a geometry or instancing bug for far too long.

Found by bisecting with screenshots rather than rebuilding: place one object at a series of Z
values, screenshot each, and the boundary shows up immediately. `app.pause()` does not stop the
loop (it kept overwriting the probe) — `__AURA3D_LIVE_APPS__.pauseAll()` does, plus neutralising
`node.setInstances` to hold a value.

**Fix: straight-down `camera.orthographic({ position: [0,12,ARENA_Z], target: [0,0,ARENA_Z],
orthographicSize: 4.3 })`.** Correct for a brick-breaker anyway — no perspective distortion, the
ring is a true circle, and what you see is where the ball actually is.

Second bug found in the same pass: **`primitives.torus` is authored in the XY plane** and renders as
a flat bar from a top-down camera until rotated `[-PI/2, 0, 0]`.

Also learned: `model()` normalises a GLB to a unit cube before node scale, so node scale is a
world-space extent, not a ratio against `assets.x.bounds`. Dividing by the bounds produced a plate
standing edge-on to the camera — which reads exactly like "the model won't render".

Final state: full arena, concentric rings, paddle arc visible and orbiting, 3 shards, waves,
scoring, strikes, restart. 25 draw calls, zero console errors, typecheck clean. All of the above is
written into `arcade/BRIEF.md` so Backspin and Vector Nine don't pay for it again.

### Typecheck trap
`tsc --noEmit` gets SIGKILLed by the sandbox at default heap size (exit 137, no output). Cap it:
`NODE_OPTIONS=--max-old-space-size=1024 tsc --noEmit`. This passes clean.

### Environment traps (also recorded in arcade/BRIEF.md)
- Playwright capture must use `/opt/homebrew/bin/node`; the managed node hits a broker IPC error.
- Chromium needs `--use-angle=metal`; software rasterizer gets SIGKILLed on memory pressure.
- Do not wrap 2-3 minute builds in `timeout 150` — you SIGTERM your own build.
- Untracked files under `arcade/` and `docs/prompts/` were wiped once mid-session. Assets under
  the gitignored `artifacts/` survived. Backup lives at `artifacts/arcade-backup/`.
